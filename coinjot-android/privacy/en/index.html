Coinjot — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

Coinjot has no account and sends no records to any LuraAI server. Receipts are read on your device, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

1. Information we collect

LuraAI ("we") does not collect personal information through Coinjot (the "app"). There is no sign-up or login, we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and the app has no analytics, tracking or advertising libraries. The app contains no code that sends your records to our servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays only on your device: expense and income records (amount, merchant, memo, date, category), ledgers, categories, monthly budget and recurring expenses; receipt images (a thumbnail, the original, or nothing, depending on the option you choose); app settings (theme, app lock, reminders, profile name and the badge you pick); and achievement badges. Also stored only on your device: the text recognized from each receipt and its image, kept as evidence (the source line the amount came from, how it was checked and the fields you edited); purchased items with quantity, unit price and discount, and the price book built from them; return and warranty deadlines; accuracy statistics (the share of entries saved without editing the amount); the exchange rate and daily travel budget you enter yourself; and images or text sent with Share to Coinjot until you confirm or discard them.

3. Receipt text and voice

Receipt text recognition uses Google ML Kit models bundled in the app and runs on your device. The camera-based document scanner is provided by Google Play services and also scans on your device; photos are not uploaded by Coinjot. For voice entry the app asks Android's speech recognition service to listen after you tap the microphone. Depending on your device and installed language packs, that system service (for example Google's) may process audio on the device or send it to its own servers under its own privacy policy; Coinjot never records or stores audio and only receives the recognized text. Anything read automatically is shown on a confirmation screen with its source text and is saved only after you confirm. Live scan shows a camera preview and reads the total line on your device with Google ML Kit; frames are not uploaded. Handwriting input (experimental) uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink on your device: the handwriting language model is downloaded once through Google Play services the first time you use it, and your strokes are never stored or sent anywhere. Fix by voice uses the same Android speech recognition service as voice entry. Questions you ask your ledger are understood on your device by rules, and the numbers in the answer are calculated by the app from your own records; nothing is sent to a server.

4. Backup and transfer

Records stay on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup (stored in your Google account under Google's terms). You can also export a backup file and import it on another device from Settings. If you use the Wear OS app, summaries and records you enter on the watch are exchanged only between your phone and your watch through the Wearable Data Layer; records from the watch are saved after you confirm them on the phone. That backup includes the app's database (records, items, evidence, price book, deadlines), receipt images and settings.

5. Device permissions

Camera: requested only when you open live scan; the document scanner and the photo picker need no permission. Microphone: used only when you tap the microphone button for voice entry or Fix by voice. Notifications: used only for return and warranty deadline reminders (requested when you add a deadline) and the optional month-end report reminder; reminders are scheduled on your device without the exact-alarm permission. Biometrics / screen lock: used only to unlock the app when app lock is on; biometric data is never given to the app. The app does not request contacts, location, SMS or storage permissions. The INTERNET permission is declared by Google Play components (billing, scanner, model download) used by the app; Coinjot does not use it to send your records anywhere.

6. Purchases

Lifetime access (one-time purchase) and monthly or yearly subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment and validation are handled by Google Play; we receive no payment information. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

7. Sharing and selling

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share with or sell to third parties, and the app uses no advertising ID. When you export a CSV file or share a monthly report as a PDF, the file is created on your device and handed to Android's share sheet; you decide where it goes. An evidence bundle (ZIP) exported from the receipt vault works the same way: it is made on your device and you choose where it goes.

8. Children

Coinjot is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. Deleting data

Settings › Delete all data removes all records, receipt images and badges on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the device. Backups kept in your Google account can be managed in your Android backup settings. This also removes evidence, items, the price book, deadlines and their scheduled reminders, and accuracy statistics.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and important changes are announced in the release notes.
