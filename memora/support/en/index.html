Getting started

What should I do first?

On first launch Memora offers “Choose starter decks”: travel, languages, education, history, general knowledge, K-culture and more. Pick some and start studying, or tap “+” to add your own cards. You can also get starter decks later from the Decks tab › Starter decks.

Can I import from Anki or CSV?

In the Decks tab › Import you can bring in Anki (.apkg), CSV/TSV files or pasted text. Check the column roles, and review the list of look-alike cards the app would skip. Study schedules are carried over approximately. The newer Anki .anki21b format is not supported — export from Anki with “Support older Anki versions”. Importing is available during the trial or with Pro.

Can I make cards from photos or documents?

In the New card screen choose “Create from documents or photos” and add a PDF, DOCX, PPTX, text file or photo. Memora drafts key terms, cards and quizzes; everything happens on the device and only the cards you pick are saved. After the trial, 3 documents a day are free.

Studying

How do the rating buttons work?

Look at the card, tap “Show answer”, then choose Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Each button shows the interval until the next review. If you tapped the wrong one, use “Undo last rating”.

How many new cards a day?

As many as the deck’s “New cards per day” setting (20 by default), minus the new cards you already started today. When you study several decks together, the total stays within the largest deck limit.

Can I restore deleted cards?

Deleted cards and decks stay in Decks tab › Recently deleted for 30 days.

Free and Pro

What is free, and what is Pro?

You get every feature for the first 14 days. After that, reviewing stays free, and so do up to 150 cards you create (starter decks do not count). Import, similar-card warnings, confusable-pair contrast, cluster study, search by meaning and AI deck creation are Pro. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase, not a subscription.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap “Restore purchase” on the Pro screen, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com

AI features

AI deck creation does not work.

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence and it works right away. Otherwise, download a Qwen3 model (about 1 GB or 2.6 GB) once over Wi-Fi. If storage or memory is low, try the smaller model. All generation happens on the device.

Are AI-made cards accurate?

Not always. Smaller models in particular can make factual mistakes. Check cards before saving, and verify medical, legal, tax and investment topics against official sources.

Generation is slow.

It depends on your device. Close other apps and keep Memora in the foreground while it runs. The 4B model is recommended only for devices with plenty of memory.

iCloud and backup

How do I sync with my other devices?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and keep Settings › iCloud sync on. Cards and study history sync; documents, quizzes and analysis data are rebuilt on each device. If sync stalls, check your iCloud sign-in and storage.

My cards disappeared.

The device makes a safety backup about once a day. If you have no cards and a backup exists, Memora offers to restore it, and you can also restore manually from Settings › iCloud sync › Restore from backup.

Where is my data?

Only on your device and in your own iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I study on Apple Watch?

Yes, today’s cards can be reviewed on an Apple Watch paired with your iPhone. If the app is missing, check the Watch app on your iPhone to install Memora.
