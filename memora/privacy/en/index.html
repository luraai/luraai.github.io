Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 October 2026

Memora has no accounts and sends none of your cards or study history to LuraAI. Embeddings, AI deck creation and document analysis all run on your device. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

1. What we collect

LuraAI ("we") collects no personal information through Memora (the "app").

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never ask for or collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking frameworks and ship no third-party SDKs.
  • The app contains no code that sends your cards or study history to us.

2. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is needed for the app to work:

  • Decks, cards, review history (when you reviewed and how you rated), and analysis results such as clusters and confusable pairs
  • Embeddings (numeric vectors) used to compare the meaning of cards. They are computed on the device and are not synced.
  • Text read from documents or photos when you create cards from them, and quizzes you generate (kept on the device only)
  • App settings (theme, fonts, read-aloud voices and so on), your trial start date and Pro entitlement
  • Safety backup files on the device (decks, cards and review history; the latest three)

3. iCloud sync — your storage, not ours

If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud sync is on in Settings (the default), decks, cards, review history and analysis results are stored in your own private iCloud database (CloudKit) and synced between your devices. We cannot access it. Small settings such as read-aloud voices and theme, and the trial start date, use iCloud key-value storage. Embeddings, document text and quizzes are not synced. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings › iCloud sync.

4. When the app uses the network

The app does not send your cards to any server. It uses the network only to:

  • Download model files: the embedding model for semantic analysis (about 165 MB) and, if you choose, a Qwen3 model for AI deck creation (about 1 GB or 2.6 GB) are downloaded once from Hugging Face. This is an ordinary file download; no cards or study content is sent. Hugging Face’s own privacy policy applies to that request (for example, your IP address).
  • Purchases: buying the lifetime unlock is handled by Apple’s App Store. We never receive your payment details.
  • iCloud sync: as described in section 3, only to Apple’s services.

5. AI features

Finding duplicate and confusable cards, search by meaning, creating cards and quizzes from documents, and creating decks with AI all run on the device, using Apple Intelligence’s on-device model (Foundation Models) or the models you download (EmbeddingGemma, Qwen3). The topics and documents you enter never leave the device. AI-made cards can be wrong, so please check them before saving; for medical, legal, tax or investment topics in particular, verify against official sources.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only when you photograph textbook pages to draft cards. Photos are used on the device to read the text and are not stored.
  • Photos and files: only the photos and documents you choose are read.
  • Read-aloud uses the device’s system voices; the app does not use the microphone.

Declining a permission only disables that feature.

7. Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app exchanges only today’s study cards and your review results with your paired iPhone. No server is involved.

8. Sharing with third parties

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to sell or share.

9. Deleting your data

Delete all data in Settings › My data removes this device’s decks, cards, review history, documents, quizzes and on-device safety backups. Deleting the app also removes the data on the device. Data stored in iCloud can be managed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Cards you delete inside the app can be restored from Recently Deleted for 30 days.

10. Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes are announced in the app update notes.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@luraai.io
