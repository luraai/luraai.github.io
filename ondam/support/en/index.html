Getting started

What do I do first?

On the Today screen tap 'New session', enter a client pseudonym and type, open the session, choose a consent method, collect both consents (recording and health data) and tap 'Stamp consent · Start recording'.

Which devices and languages does Ondam support?

Ondam runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The app screens follow your device's language and are available in 11 languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, Malay and Thai.

How do I choose the counseling (session) language?

The session language is separate from the screen language. Choose it in Settings, or per session when you create it. It sets the language used for speech recognition, the AI draft, anonymization and crisis-phrase rules, and search.

Can I use it without recording?

Choose 'Oral after session' as the consent method. Nothing is recorded and you write the note by hand afterwards.

Recording and speech recognition

Recording does not start.

Check Settings > Ondam and make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed.

No live transcript appears.

Install the speech recognition for your session language (listed as ' speech recognition (live transcript)') on the Security & Retention screen. The language data is downloaded by the system. If it is unavailable or the device does not support it, the app records only and converts the audio afterwards with the fallback recognizer (download its model on the same screen, after you confirm).

Are speakers separated automatically?

No. Tap the counselor / client / guardian button when the speaker changes.

AI drafts

How are drafts made?

Apple Intelligence (on device) writes first; if it is unavailable or declines, a downloaded open-source model writes; if neither is available a rule-based skeleton is used. The review screen shows which step wrote the draft.

How good are the drafts in my language?

Draft quality varies by language and by the model used. A draft is a pencil draft: it can be wrong or incomplete, and the counselor must review, edit and confirm it before it becomes part of the record.

A draft is empty or some sentences are missing.

Sentences with no support in the conversation, and sentences that read like a diagnosis or risk rating, are removed. Add anything missing with 'Rewrite'.

Why is Assessment (A) empty?

Only the counselor writes the assessment; the AI never does. Once you write 5 or more characters you can seal the note.

How do I download an open-source model?

Security & Retention > Local open-source models > Download. A confirmation appears, and the counter at the top rises only while downloading. We recommend Qwen3 4B for devices with 8 GB or more of memory and Kanana 1.5 2.1B for 6 GB devices. Apart from this download and the system's speech recognition language data, nothing leaves the device. You can delete models at any time.

Subscription

What requires a subscription?

Recording new sessions and creating transcripts and drafts. There is a 1-month free trial. Viewing, exporting to PDF and deleting existing records never requires a subscription.

How do I cancel?

Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > Ondam. Cancelling during the free trial means you are not charged.

My subscription is not restored.

Tap 'Restore purchases' on the subscription screen, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Records and retention

Where are records stored?

Only on this device, encrypted. Nothing is uploaded to a server or iCloud.

Can I move records to a new device?

No, because the encryption key is bound to the device. Export any record you need to keep as a PDF.

When are records deleted?

When the retention period you chose for the session (1, 3 or 5 years) ends, records are deleted automatically on app launch. You can delete earlier with 'Delete now' in the archive. Content cannot be recovered; only the hash and date remain.

What does the 'needs review' flag mean?

When the conversation contains a phrase such as 'I want to die', that phrase is quoted verbatim and flagged. The app does not assess or score risk; review and response are the counselor's responsibility.
