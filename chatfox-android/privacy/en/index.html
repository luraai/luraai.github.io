Your resume never leaves this device.

What is collected

Nothing. Chatfox has no accounts, no servers, and ships no analytics or advertising SDKs. The resumes, portfolios, and job postings you add, the questions and answers generated from them, and the transcripts and pronunciation scores from spoken answers are all stored on this device and are never transmitted.

When the app uses the network

Two, and only two. (1) The first time you use the app it downloads the AI model files from huggingface.co. The only thing sent is a request for those files; none of your content goes with it. (2) Purchasing or restoring Chatfox Pro talks to Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google Play; Chatfox never sees them.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only during a voice mock interview. Recognition runs only through Android's on-device speech recognition (server-based recognition is never used); if the device has no offline speech language you can answer by text instead. Audio is never saved to a file: it stays in memory only until its pronunciation score is computed.

Backup

If Android Auto Backup is on, your practice history and settings can be included in your own Google account backup. Google stores that backup; the developers of Chatfox cannot access it. The downloaded AI model is never part of the backup. You can also export your history as a file in Settings.

Diagnostics

The app includes no crash-reporting tool or remote diagnostics. The basic stability statistics in the Google Play Console are collected by Google only when you have usage and diagnostics sharing turned on for your device.

Widgets and watch

Only a short summary for widgets and your Wear OS watch (streak, this week's count, recent scores, today's question text, and an interview date and name) is stored on this device and handed to your own watch over the Wearable Data Layer. It never includes your resume, documents or answers, and it is never transmitted anywhere else. You can turn off question and interview display on widgets in Settings, and deleting your history clears this summary too.

Deleting your data

You can delete your history and your working documents at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything from the device, including the downloaded model.

Other details

  • Advertising and tracking. None. Chatfox contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs.
  • Children. Chatfox is not directed to children under 13 (18+ rating suggested) and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
  • Where the data lives. Documents you add, generated questions and answers, transcripts, scores and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is stored on servers operated by the developer.
  • Model downloads. AI model files are downloaded from huggingface.co (the Qwen3 models are published under the Apache-2.0 license). Only the request for the file is sent; the file is checked for size and integrity on the device.
  • Notifications. If you turn on the daily practice reminder, the app posts a local notification on this device. The text never contains your documents or answers.
  • Wear OS. The optional watch app sends a short recording of your answer from the watch to your own phone for analysis; the recording is deleted after analysis. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • Your rights. Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or erase on our side. You control everything on your device: delete history or all data in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Changes. If this policy changes we will update the date above and the in-app text.
