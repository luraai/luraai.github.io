Frequently asked questions

How big is the AI model download and do I need Wi-Fi?

The default Qwen3 1.7B model is about 1 GB. The 4B model is about 2.7 GB and the 8B model about 4.9 GB. Download on Wi-Fi if you can; on mobile data the app asks you to confirm. Downloads can be paused and resumed. After the download the app works without internet.

Which model does my phone use?

It depends on memory. Devices with under about 3.5 GB RAM use the built-in offline coach (rule-based, no download). 4-6 GB devices use the small model, 6-10 GB devices can also use the medium model, and 10 GB or more can also run the large model. On supported phones you can choose Gemini Nano (provided by Android, no download inside the app) under Settings > Models.

What is the offline coach?

A rule-based coach that needs no model. It asks template questions and gives checklist-style feedback. It is not generative AI.

Voice interview says it cannot recognise speech.

Chatfox uses only Android's on-device speech recognition so your voice never leaves the phone. Install the offline speech pack for your language: Settings > System > Languages > Voice input (the exact path depends on the phone brand). Android 12 or later is required. You can always answer by typing instead.

The pronunciation score looks odd.

Scores are a guide built from recognition confidence, speaking pace, filler words and pitch variation. They are not phoneme-level grading. Quiet rooms and a steady distance from the microphone give the most reliable numbers.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes, after the one-time model download (or with the offline coach from the start). Buying or restoring Pro needs a connection to Google Play.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings > Restore purchase (shown while you are on the free plan). Use the same Google account you bought with.

How do I delete my data?

Settings > Delete all sessions removes your history and working documents. Uninstalling the app removes everything, including downloaded models. Android Auto Backup may keep a copy in your own Google backup; manage it in your phone's Google backup settings.

Is anything uploaded?

No. There are no accounts and no servers. The only network traffic is the model file download from huggingface.co and the Google Play purchase flow. See the privacy policy.

How do I use the Wear OS watch app?

Install Chatfox on your watch from Google Play. It shows your streak and today's question, can read the question aloud, and records a short answer that your phone analyses (the phone app must be installed and nearby). The tile and complications show your streak and weekly goal.
