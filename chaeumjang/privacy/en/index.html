Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 October 2026 · 한국어

Chaeumjang has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics. Your documents and records stay on this device and are never sent to LuraAI or any third party. The only internet use is downloading the model file for automatic document sorting, and only if you agree.

1. What we collect

LuraAI (“we”) collects no personal information through Chaeumjang (the “app”).

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never ask for or collect a name, email, phone number, location, device identifier or usage history.
  • We use no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs and ship no third-party SDKs.
  • The app has no feature that sends your documents or entries to our servers.

2. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is needed for the app to work:

  • Contracts and forms you fill in, your business profile and clients
  • Your seal settings and signature images (drawn, or imported from a photo)
  • Imported and scanned documents, their stamping history, and text read from them (a cache used for sorting and review)
  • Ledger entries, receipt photos and app settings

Deleting the app removes all of it, and you can erase everything yourself in Settings › “Delete all data”.

3. Device permissions

Declining a permission only disables the feature that needs it.

PermissionWhy
CameraScanning paper documents and photographing receipts. Photos stay on this device.
Photo libraryOnly when you pick a receipt or signature photo to import.

The app does not ask for notification permission.

4. AI on the device

AI contract review runs on Apple Intelligence’s on-device model (Foundation Models). The text of the document being reviewed is processed on the device and is not sent to us or any third party. It needs a supported device, iOS 26 or later, and a supported language.

Automatic document sorting runs an open model (embeddinggemma-2, about 310 MB) on the device. After you start the app, the model file is downloaded once from Hugging Face only if you agree. Only the model file is downloaded; none of your documents or information is uploaded. As with any download, the server (Hugging Face) can see your IP address. You can delete the model in Settings at any time, and it is excluded from device backups.

5. Exports and backups

PDF, HWPX and DOCX files and backup files (.chaeumbackup) are saved only where you choose (Files, Share Sheet, etc.). A backup contains your documents and signature images and is not encrypted, so take care where you keep or share it. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send it.

6. Purchases

The app is free to download. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase (an in-app purchase, not a subscription). Payment and receipt validation are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive payment details or Apple Account information. You can restore the purchase with “Restore Purchase” on the Pro screen.

7. Sharing, ads and tracking

We collect no personal information, so there is none to share or sell. The app uses no cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking.

8. Children

Chaeumjang collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes are announced in the app update notes.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@luraai.io
