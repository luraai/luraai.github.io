Getting started

How do I write a contract?

On the Contracts tab pick a template and fill in the blanks; the document builds live (on the Preview tab on iPhone). Required clauses stay, and you switch optional clauses on or off. Fill in My info once and your business details are filled in for you.

How do I make my seal?

On the Seals & Signatures tab, under Seals, choose a name, shape, layout and ink colour. On a template contract tap “Stamp”; on an imported PDF tap the seal in the tray at the bottom.

How do I stamp an imported PDF?

Under Contracts › My documents, use “Import a document” or “Scan a paper document”, then open the PDF. Tap a seal to place it, drag to move, pull a corner to resize and use the top handle to rotate. The file does not change until you save; the first save keeps your original so “Remove all stamps and restore the original” can bring it back.

Signatures

How do I save a signature?

On Seals & Signatures › Signatures, write with your finger or Apple Pencil, or import one from a photo. Your first signature is free to save; keeping two or more needs Pro. “Sign here” in the stamping tray lets you sign once without saving.

How do I add my signature to a template contract?

In the contract editor open the menu (…) › “Add signature” and pick a saved one. It is placed under the signature line when you export to PDF, Hangul or Word.

Seam and side-by-side stamps (Pro)

How do I stamp across two pages?

Drag a seal so it straddles the edge between two pages to make a seam stamp. To stamp two contracts side by side, go to My documents, choose “Stamp two documents together” and pick two PDFs. Both are Pro features.

Pro and purchases

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: templates and export, making seals, stamping one document, saving one signature, the ledger and taxes. Pro (one-time purchase, for life, not a subscription): several saved signatures, seam and side-by-side stamps, AI contract review and automatic document sorting.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Tap “Restore Purchase” on the Pro screen, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com

AI features (Pro)

AI review is missing or not working.

It needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, iOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings, and a supported language (Korean). If it cannot run, the app tells you why. All AI runs on the device, and the review is not legal advice.

What is the sorting model?

A model file (about 310 MB) that sorts imported documents for you. After you agree it is downloaded once, and you can delete it any time in Settings › Document sorting model. Your documents never leave the device. Without the model, simple keyword rules are used.

Backup and data

Where is my data?

Only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I move to another device?

Create a backup file in Settings › Device backup and open it on the new device. Imported PDF files are not part of the backup, so move them separately (for example with the Files app).
