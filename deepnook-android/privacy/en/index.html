Privacy Policy — Deepnook

Effective: October 16, 2026

Deepnook has no account and no server. Your sessions, notes and recaps are processed and stored only on your device. The AI features run on your device too. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Information we collect

None. LuraAI ("we") does not collect personal information through Deepnook ("the app").

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and does not use the advertising ID.
  • Sessions, notes, tags, recaps and settings are stored only on your device. We do not operate a server that receives them.

2. AI features

The numbers in recaps and mentor tips are calculated by the app. On supported phones, an on-device language model (Gemini Nano through Android AICore) may polish the wording; this runs on your device. Your notes are used only if "Quote notes in AI recap" is on, and notes that look like they mention self-harm are never given to the model. On other devices, or in unsupported languages, the app builds the text from templates.

3. Focus sounds

Focus and break sounds are generated by the app in real time on your device. The app has no audio files, does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

4. Notifications, widget and watch

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; there is no push server. During a session a notification shows the time left. The widget reads the same on-device data. If you use the Wear OS app, session status is exchanged directly between your phone and watch through Google's Wearable Data Layer.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the time left and tell you when a session ends.
  • Exact alarms and foreground service: so a session ends on time and the timer keeps running with the screen off.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): used only if you turn on the setting yourself, to quiet notifications during a session.

The app does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

6. Purchases

Deepnook Pro (monthly and yearly subscriptions and a lifetime purchase) is sold through Google Play. Google processes payment; the app never receives your payment details. The app checks your purchases on the device through Google Play Billing.

7. Backup

If Android automatic backup is on, your records and settings may be saved in your Google account backup. Google keeps that backup and we cannot access it. You can also export or import a file in Settings.

8. Selling and sharing

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share or sell, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

9. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Deletion

In Settings you can export your data and delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes all data on the device. Backups in your Google account can be deleted in your device's backup settings.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and significant changes are noted in the app's update notes.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@luraai.io
