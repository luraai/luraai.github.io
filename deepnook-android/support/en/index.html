Using Deepnook

How do I start a session?

Pull the cord at the top right of the Home screen, or press and hold it. Set the length with the ruler first. During a session the time left also shows in the notification shade and on the lock screen.

What happens if I end a session early?

Sessions of 10 seconds or less are treated as accidents and are not recorded. Longer ones are saved as ended early. Nothing is graded.

Does Deepnook block other apps?

No. If you switch to another app during a focus session, the time pauses and a gentle alert asks you to come back; locking your screen still counts as focus time.

A notification stays up during a session.

It shows the time left (a foreground service). It disappears when the session ends or you end it.

Notifications and battery

The session ended but I got no notification.

Check that notifications are on in Settings > Apps > Deepnook > Notifications. On phones with aggressive battery saving, setting Deepnook to unrestricted battery use can help.

What is the Do Not Disturb link?

Turn on "Do Not Disturb while focusing" in Settings and allow Do Not Disturb access on the system screen. While the light is on, notifications are limited to priority only, and restored when the session ends.

Deepnook Pro

What is included in Pro?

AI mentor tips, 3 themes, focus and break sounds, full-history stats, all widgets and past recaps. Choose monthly, yearly (free trial if eligible) or lifetime.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Cancel in the Google Play app > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. To restore, open Deepnook > Settings > Restore purchases with the same Google account. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Why can't I see AI recaps or mentor tips written by AI?

On-device AI needs a supported phone and language. Otherwise Deepnook shows the same statistics with template wording.

The sound doesn't play.

Open the speaker button during a session and pick a sound. Sounds stop when a session pauses or ends, and when headphones are unplugged.

Data and privacy

Where is my data?

Only on your device (plus your Google account backup if automatic backup is on). Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I export, import or delete my data?

Settings > Backup and export lets you export CSV/JSON or import JSON. Settings > Delete all data erases everything.

Can the watch app work without the phone?

The Wear OS app remotely controls Deepnook on your phone, so the phone app is required.
