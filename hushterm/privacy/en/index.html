Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 October 2026 · 한국어

Hushterm has no accounts and sends nothing to LuraAI servers. Your commands and terminal output travel only between your device and the server you choose, and AI runs on the device by default. Only if you connect your own AI provider does data go from your device directly to that provider. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

1. What we collect

LuraAI (“we”) collects no personal information through Hushterm (the “app”).

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never ask for or collect a name, email, phone number or location.
  • We use no analytics, tracking or advertising frameworks and ship no third-party SDKs.
  • The app contains no networking code that sends data to us.

2. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is needed for the app to work:

  • Host details (name, address, port, username, tag), folders, port forwards, snippets and recent commands
  • SSH passwords and private keys, kept in the iOS Keychain. Secure Enclave keys cannot leave the device, and private keys never leave it.
  • Host-key fingerprints of servers you have connected to (confirmed on first use)
  • Server-check reports (the last few per host), the history of AI-agent requests and your decisions, your own server checks, and practice-course progress
  • App settings (themes, key bar, scrollback, app lock and so on) and your Pro entitlement
  • Playbooks, the operations log (the deck’s history), fleet question results and log lens results
  • AI provider settings (provider, address, model, and your consent for each provider and address). API keys for your AI provider are stored only in the device Keychain and are never synced to iCloud.

3. Server connections and terminal content

SSH and SFTP connections go directly from your device to the server you specify; no LuraAI relay is involved. We cannot see terminal output or the files you transfer. What happens on the server is governed by that server’s operator.

Server checks. A server check runs only read-only commands (from a fixed allow-list, or ones you wrote and that passed the same allow-list) on the server you chose, over your own SSH connection. The results are judged on the device and kept on the device; nothing is sent to us or to any other party, except a summary you send to your own AI provider as described in section 4. The Linux practice course runs on a simulated server inside the app and uses no network.

4. AI features and voice input

By default, command suggestions, error analysis, output summaries, connection diagnosis, log lens explanations, answers to fleet questions and playbook explanations are produced on the device by Apple Intelligence’s on-device model; when it is not available, the app shows sentences written by rules on the device. The demo model used on the virtual servers inside the app runs fixed scenarios built into the app and does not use the network. Risky-command decisions and check verdicts are made by a rule engine on the device, not by a model. Voice input uses on-device speech recognition only.

An AI provider you connect yourself (optional, Pro). Only if you connect Anthropic (Claude), an OpenAI-compatible service or a model on your own server with your own API key in Settings › Built-in agent, the following data is sent directly from your device to that provider when you use the built-in agent or an incident investigation, or when the app writes the explanation for a fleet question, the log lens or a playbook: the goal or question you typed; the server’s name, operating system and whether it is production; the output of the read-only commands the app ran and of the commands you approved; and summaries of check and log results. Before sending, the app automatically masks secrets such as private keys, tokens and passwords, but it cannot recognize every secret. This transfer does not pass through our servers, and we cannot see its content. How the provider handles the data it receives is governed by that provider’s terms and privacy policy. Before first use, the app shows the provider, the address and the data that will be sent and asks for your consent (recorded for each provider and address); you can withdraw consent and delete the key in Settings. If you connect a server running on your own network or on this device, the data does not go out to the internet. If you do not connect a provider, no data is sent to any third-party AI service.

5. iCloud backup — your storage, not ours

If you turn on iCloud backup, host details, port forwards, snippets and folders are stored in your own Apple account through Apple’s iCloud key-value store. We cannot access it. SSH passwords sync through iCloud Keychain only if you turn on “Also keep passwords in iCloud Keychain” (off by default); private keys are never synced. You can turn it off at any time in Settings › iCloud.

6. AI agent approvals, widgets and Apple Watch

When an AI coding agent such as Claude Code or Codex that runs inside your own SSH session asks for permission, Hushterm detects the prompt in the terminal text on the device and shows Approve / Deny buttons in the app. Hushterm itself never contacts those agents or their providers. With Pro, a local notification may appear on the device for a short time after you leave the app. Approval notifications and the Live Activity are generated locally on the device; no push server is used. Your host list and approval requests go between iPhone and Apple Watch only and do not pass through any server.

8. Clipboard

The paste guard fixes smart quotes and dashes and asks before a multi-line paste, at the moment you paste, on the device. It does not store or transmit clipboard contents.

9. In-app purchases

Monthly and yearly subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold through Apple’s StoreKit. Payment and receipt validation are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive payment details or Apple Account information. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

10. Sharing and selling

We collect no personal information, and we do not sell or share your data. The only exception is data you choose to send to your own AI provider, as described in section 4. The app uses no cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking.

11. Encryption

The app uses only standard SSH encryption.

12. Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

13. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes hosts, settings and Keychain items from the device. Data backed up to iCloud can be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes are announced in the app update notes.
