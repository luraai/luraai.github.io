Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Banchanbell is an offline game with no account and no server. We collect no personal information, and your progress is stored only on your device.

1. Information we collect

LuraAI (“we”) does not collect any personal information through Banchanbell (“the app”) — no name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. There is no sign-up, the app does not talk to any server we operate, and it contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDK.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your game progress (ingredients, orders, jar state, coins, stars, energy and settings) is stored on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also use “Reset all data” in Settings.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases such as energy packs and the Starter Pack are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. You can review purchases and request refunds in your Apple account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

4. Notifications

Notifications are off by default. Only if you turn them on in Settings does the app schedule a local “your jar is ripe” notification on your device. No server push is used.

5. Advertising

The current version contains no advertising SDK. If we later add optional rewarded ads, we will update this policy and the app’s privacy details first and provide consent prompts (ATT/UMP).

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will post the new date here. Contact: contact@luraai.io
