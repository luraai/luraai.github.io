FAQ and how to reach us

What is the onggi jar?

Put one tier 3–5 ingredient in a jar and it ripens into the next tier over time. Pull it early for unripe (★1), at the right time for perfect (★), a bit late for well-aged (★2), and much too late it becomes an ‘Aged …’ dish. Aged dishes are never lost — special orders ask for them. See Menu > Rules & odds in the game for the timetable.

I ran out of energy

Energy refills 1 every 3 minutes. Merging, delivering and jars cost no energy. You can buy energy in the shop if you like, but it is never required.

How do I restore purchases?

Settings > Restore purchases. The one-time Starter Pack ownership is restored; its energy is not granted again. Consumable energy packs are not restorable.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new device

Progress is stored on your device only (no account, no server). It moves with an iCloud or Finder backup restore.

Contact

Bugs and suggestions: contact@luraai.io. Please include your device model, iOS version and what happened.
