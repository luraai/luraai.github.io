FAQ

Does it work without a signal?
Yes. Capture, hashing, signatures, sealing and PDF export all work offline. Timestamps are added automatically once the network returns.

Timestamps say "pending".
The time-stamp authority could not be reached yet. The app retries automatically with growing delays. One timestamp also shows that every earlier entry existed before that time. The hash chain and device signatures are valid in the meantime.

A warning says my device clock was wrong.
At capture time the device clock was ahead of the time-stamp server. For those entries, trust the timestamp time rather than the time written by the device. Turn on Set Automatically in Settings > General > Date & Time.

Can I edit a sealed unit?
Original photos cannot change. After sealing, notes and status changes go on the annotation layer and photos taken after a repair are added as follow-up records.

How do repair reminders work?
Set a follow-up date on a defect and you are reminded the day before and on the day at 9:00. It is your own schedule, not a legal deadline. Requires the Follow-up Pass.

How does before/after comparison work?
After sealing a unit, take new photos with “Photo after repair” on the defect screen and compare them side by side. The photos from before the seal never change.

Can I mark up a photo?
Open the photo, tap “Mark” and draw with Apple Pencil or a finger. Marks are stored on a separate layer above the photo and each mark gets its own ledger entry; the original photo and its hash never change. In the PDF the marked picture appears with a “hand marks included” note.

Which report formats exist?
On Mac choose Full, Claim (only defects that are not yet repaired) or Follow-up (only defects with photos taken after repair). All three carry the same ledger and seal information.

How do I check the timestamp of a bundle I received?
Open the bundle in the verifier of HajaNote for Mac to see the entry table and chain status, check the timestamp token's signature and save it as a .tst file. Tokens from an authority the system does not trust are shown as such. Certificate revocation and expiry are not checked.

No AI draft appears.
Check Apple Intelligence status or download an open-source model in Settings > AI Models. Rule-based drafts are always available.

How do voice defect entry, the summary draft and defect search work?
Tap the microphone button at the bottom of a unit and say, for example, "living room wallpaper lifting, two spots". The app suggests a space, trade and defect type, and records only what you confirm. "Summary and letter draft" in the unit menu is built from the defect counts and is not part of the record or its hashes. In the search field you can type everyday phrases such as "water leaking". These three features are part of the Self-inspection Kit and run entirely on the device. Photo-quality hints (blurry, dark, duplicate) are free.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone (iOS 26 or later), iPad (iPadOS 26 or later), Mac (macOS 26 or later) and Apple Watch (watchOS 26 or later). The Mac app is for opening and verifying bundles and building reports.

What is a .hjn file?
Export it from a unit and send it by AirDrop or any messenger. Open it in HajaNote on Mac or another iPhone to verify and merge. It contains the unit number and signer names in clear, so send it only to the intended recipient.

I got a new phone / lost my phone.
The key that opens your records exists only on the device, so you need the backup file (.hjnx) you made in Settings > Backup and the password you chose. On the new phone use Settings > Import .hjn bundle / backup, pick the file and enter the password. The password is not stored and cannot be recovered.

How do I delete my data?
Long-press a unit and delete it: its records and photo files are removed immediately. Deleting the app removes everything. PDFs and bundles you already sent cannot be deleted.

What is free and what is paid?
Capture, hashing, timestamps, sealing, and verifying or opening bundles are free. One unit and PDFs with a trial mark are free; the Self-inspection Kit (KRW 9,900, one-time) removes the unit limit and the mark. The Follow-up Pass (KRW 19,900, 2 years, no auto-renewal) adds repair reminders and follow-up photos. Restore purchases in Settings > Pass.

Do reports have legal force?
Reports document when records were made and the hash of each original. Criteria and warranty-period notes are references only. Whether something is a defect is decided by the defect dispute committee or a court.
