HajaNote Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · LuraAI (contact@luraai.io)

1. What we collect

HajaNote has no developer server. Photos, dictated text, notes, unit numbers, signatures (name and drawing), complex names, your company logo and marks drawn on photos with Apple Pencil or a finger stay on your device. We cannot see or collect them. Voice is not kept; only the transcribed text is.

2. What leaves your device

  • Timestamp requests: to prove when a record was made, only a SHA-256 hash is sent to an RFC 3161 time-stamp authority (FreeTSA, Sectigo). The newest hash of a unit covers every earlier entry, so one request is sent per unit, not per photo. No photos, names, locations or unit numbers are sent, and a hash cannot be reversed into the original. As with any internet request, the authority can see your device's IP address and the time of the request.
  • AI model download: if you choose, open-source model files are downloaded from Hugging Face and its file-delivery servers. None of your records are sent; the servers can see your IP address and request time.
  • System speech model: the first time you use Apple dictation, iOS may download a Korean speech model from Apple. This is done by the operating system; HajaNote does not send audio anywhere.
  • Purchases: payments for the Self-inspection Kit and the Follow-up Pass are handled by Apple (App Store). HajaNote receives no payment details; StoreKit on your device checks your purchases with Apple. The terms of use are Apple's standard End User License Agreement (EULA).
  • Mac verifier: when HajaNote on Mac checks the timestamp token of a bundle you received, the signature is computed on your Mac and the token is not sent anywhere.
  • Apple Watch: remote shutter, voice memos and seal requests travel only between your iPhone and your Apple Watch.
  • Files you share: PDF reports, .hjn bundles and backups leave your device only when you send them through the share sheet. PDFs and .hjn files can contain unit numbers and signer names; PDFs have an option to mask the unit number and signer names. A .hjn file contains the unit number in clear, so send it only to its intended recipient.

3. On-device AI

Dictation, report-sentence drafts, voice defect entry (suggesting room, trade and type), and inspection summary and request-letter drafts run on your device with Apple Intelligence or open-source models you downloaded. Defect search and photo-quality hints (blur, brightness, duplicates) are computed on the device without a model; no index or analysis result is stored. Photos and audio are not sent to any AI service. AI output is recorded as a note only after you confirm it, and summary and letter drafts are not part of the record or its hashes. A voice memo sent from Apple Watch is kept temporarily on the iPhone as transcribed text until you confirm it, and you can discard it without adding it.

4. Permissions

Camera (defect photos), microphone / speech recognition (voice memos) and notifications (optional repair follow-up reminders). Notification text shows the room and defect number, never the unit number. You can turn each off in Settings.

5. Storage, backup and deletion

Original photos and unit numbers are encrypted with AES-GCM on the device; the key lives in this device's Keychain. Signer names, complex names and notes are stored in the device's protected storage (iOS Data Protection). Because the key never leaves the device, records inside the app cannot be opened after you replace or lose the device. In Settings you can create a password-protected full backup; HajaNote does not store the password and cannot recover it.

Deleting a unit immediately removes its records, encrypted photo files and floor plan from the device (sealed units too, after a confirmation); deleting the app removes everything. PDFs, bundles and backups you already shared remain wherever you sent or stored them, and HajaNote cannot delete them.

6. Tracking and ads

HajaNote uses no ads, analytics or tracking.

7. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so you can view, correct and delete everything yourself in the app. For anything else, contact us below.
