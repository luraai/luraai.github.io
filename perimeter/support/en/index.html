Requirements

  • iOS/iPadOS 26 or later on iPhone and iPad; macOS 26 or later on Mac; watchOS 26 or later on Apple Watch.
  • Korean live dictation and on-device AI work on supported devices. Other devices use typed input and rule-based summaries.
  • Works offline (an internet connection may be needed once to download speech assets).
  • The interface is in Korean.

Getting started

  1. On the Today tab, tap + to add a meeting or import today's calendar events.
  2. When the meeting ends, tap the mic and speak a 2-minute briefing in your own voice.
  3. After "끝내기" (finish), tap "맞아요" (that's right) on the need cards that fit.
  4. Hold the Article 17 (suitability) and Article 19 (explanation) seals to stamp them.
  5. Generate the draft, stamp Article 22 (advertising review), then confirm "send via KakaoTalk".

FAQ

Dictation does not work

Check the microphone and speech recognition permissions in Settings > Perimeter. iOS downloads the Korean speech assets the first time, so try the first run on Wi-Fi. If it still fails, you can type the briefing line by line.

I cannot stamp a seal

Hold the seal for 650 ms. Article 17 needs at least two confirmed need cards (sensitive cards excluded), Article 19 needs a confirmation method, and Article 22 becomes available once the draft exists. All three seals are required before sending opens. With a hardware keyboard, Tab to the seal and hold Space or Return.

Can I stamp on Apple Watch?

No. The watch only records the briefing; seals are stamped on iPhone, iPad or Mac. Complications show seal counts and the next meeting.

What happens to the raw audio?

It is deleted as soon as processing ends and the deletion is recorded in the ledger. A temporary file left by a crash is removed on the next launch.

It says the chain does not match

Tap "다시 검증" (verify again) on the ledger screen. The message appears when a record has been altered or removed. The app offers no way to edit the ledger.

Names turn into "김○○"

This is intended. Client names are replaced with a pseudonym as soon as you enter them.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a server?

Only on your device; there is no developer server. Cross-device sync and export are not supported.

I want to erase everything

Settings > "이 기기의 모든 기록 지우기" (erase all records on this device). It cannot be undone.

Does it recommend products or give legal advice?

No. Perimeter is a record-keeping aid. Compliance with Articles 17, 19 and 22 is your own judgment and your company's standard.
