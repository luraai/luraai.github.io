Getting started

What should I do first?

Create a child profile with a nickname (a nickname is recommended instead of a real name), an age band and interests. Then pick one of the built-in stories or tap “Create story” on the Tonight tab.

Which devices can create AI stories?

An iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings and a language Apple Intelligence supports. If AI stories are not available, the Tonight tab explains why, and the eight built-in stories, narration and the Library keep working.

Which languages does the app support?

Korean, English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Vietnamese, French, Italian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Hindi and Arabic. The app, the built-in stories and the safety filters follow your device language. AI stories are available in the languages Apple Intelligence supports.

Reading and listening

How do I get a more natural voice?

Download a high-quality voice in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, then choose it in Fablenest under Settings › Narration › Voice.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap the timer on the player bar to choose 10, 15 or 30 minutes. The sound fades out over the last 5 seconds and narration stops.

Can I use my Apple Watch?

Yes. Install Fablenest on your Apple Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. From your wrist you can play and pause, skip sentences, follow the current sentence, set the sleep timer and start one of your recent stories. Narration plays from your iPhone.

Free, Pro and subscription

What is free and what needs Pro?

Free: unlimited narration of the built-in stories and one AI story per week. Fablenest Pro (a one-time purchase) and the subscription both unlock unlimited AI stories, the AI features (tonight's pick, talk questions, story ideas and bedtime-routine stories), up to 5 child profiles, sequels and PDF export.

What is the difference between Pro and the subscription?

Pro is paid once and is yours to keep; it is not a subscription. The monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Fablenest and cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I restore my purchase?

In Fablenest open Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases. You must be signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com

Safety and privacy

A story contains something I don't like.

Open the story, tap the ⋯ menu and choose “Report a problem”. The story is hidden on your device and you can choose whether to email it to us.

Is my child's information sent anywhere?

No. Profiles and stories stay on your device, and AI runs on-device. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Data › Delete all data.

How do I turn off or remove illustrations?

Open Settings › Illustrations and turn off “Draw an illustration for every scene”. Press and hold any illustration in a story to delete it or draw it again.
