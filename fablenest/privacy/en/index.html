Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 October 2026

Fablenest has no accounts and sends none of your child's information to LuraAI. Stories are written on your device by Apple Intelligence. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

1. What we collect

LuraAI (“we”) collects no personal information through Fablenest (the “app”).

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never ask for your child's photo, birth date, real name, email, phone number or location. We suggest a nickname instead of a real name.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking frameworks and ship no third-party SDKs.
  • The app contains no code that sends your child's profile or stories to us.

2. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is needed for the app to work:

  • Child profiles: nickname, age band, interests and topics to avoid (up to 5 profiles)
  • Stories you create or open: text, chapter summaries, the answers you choose for your child, favorites and reading progress
  • Illustrations that Apple's Image Playground draws on your device for story scenes (you can turn this off in Settings, and delete or redraw any illustration)
  • Settings: narration voice and speed, sleep timer, reminder time, and a local counter for the free weekly story
  • Whether you have Fablenest Pro or a subscription (provided by Apple's StoreKit)

3. AI and illustrations run on your device

AI stories and AI features (tonight's pick, talk questions, story ideas, bedtime-routine stories) use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. What you type and what the model writes stay on your device and are never sent to us or to any server. Before and after generation the app checks text against a built-in word list stored inside the app.

Illustrations are drawn on your device by Apple's Image Playground (an iOS system feature) from a short scene description that never contains your child's name. The image is stored only on your device. You can turn automatic illustrations off in Settings › Illustrations.

4. iCloud sync (optional)

Fablenest may offer an optional iCloud sync setting that is off by default. If you turn it on, your profiles and stories are stored in your own private iCloud database (CloudKit) and synced between your devices. We cannot access it. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

5. Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app exchanges story titles, the sentence being read and playback controls with your paired iPhone over the local connection between the two devices. Nothing goes through a server.

6. When the app uses the network

The app itself sends no requests to LuraAI servers. The network is used only for:

  • Purchases: Fablenest Pro and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Links you open (privacy policy, terms, support) in your browser.
  • Reporting a problem: if you report a story, the app hides it on your device and opens your mail app. You decide whether to attach the story text; it is sent from your own mail account to contact@luraai.io.

7. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): a bedtime reminder is scheduled locally on your device. It does not contain your child's name.
  • Narration uses the system voices on your device and does not use the microphone.

If you decline a permission, only that feature is unavailable.

8. Sharing with third parties

We do not collect your information, so there is nothing for us to sell or share with third parties.

9. Deleting your data

In the app, Settings › Data › Delete all data erases profiles, stories, illustrations and settings on this device. Deleting the app also removes the data on the device. Data stored in iCloud can be managed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. You can also delete a single story from the Library.

10. Children

Fablenest is for parents and guardians, not for children to use alone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app asks for none.

11. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the effective date above changes; significant changes are announced in the app update notes.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@luraai.io
