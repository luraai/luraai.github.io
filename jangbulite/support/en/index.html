Using the app

What is Jangbu Lite?

An app for small contractors that links progress photos, change-order signatures and invoices into a hash chain with timestamps, so you can later check whether any record was altered.

What is free?

One job site is free. Subscribing removes the job-site limit. Without a subscription, records you already made stay on this device and remain viewable.

Can I use it with work gloves?

Turn on Glove mode in Settings for larger buttons and text. It is on by default.

What does the Apple Watch app do?

It is a read-only app showing the balance due, warranty days left and seal alerts for change orders. Records are created only on iPhone.

Records and proof

What gets recorded when I seal?

The hash of the original photo, the capture time, and hashes of a change order's items, amount and signature image are chained to the previous record. If any record is altered, later verification flags it.

What is a timestamp?

A public timestamp authority attaches proof of time to a record's hash. Only the hash is sent, never photos or job details. When offline it is obtained automatically once you reconnect; until then the proof level reads ‘weak’.

Are sealed records legally binding?

The app only helps you confirm a record has not been altered afterwards and does not guarantee legal effect. The effect of e-signatures and electronic documents depends on the parties' agreement and applicable law, and the app is not legal advice. Consult a professional for important disputes.

What is the 15 million KRW notice?

When a contract is 15,000,000 KRW or more, the app reminds you to check registration requirements under Korea's Framework Act on the Construction Industry. The app does not decide whether you are registered.

How do warranty reminders work?

After completion, the app schedules on-device notifications 30 days and 7 days before the warranty period you entered ends. The app does not set the period; enter what your contract says.

Data and backup

Where are my records stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server or iCloud. See the privacy policy for details.

What if I change phones or delete the app?

Records exist only on the device, so deleting the app removes them and we cannot recover them. Use ‘Export backup’ in Settings and keep the file somewhere safe.

How do I send an invoice or completion report?

Create the PDF on the job screen and send it through the share sheet to KakaoTalk, Mail, Messages and more.

AI drafts

Which devices can use AI drafts?

They appear for subscribers on Apple Intelligence devices when the app language is Korean. Apple Intelligence must be turned on, and the buttons are hidden if the device does not support it. You can also turn them off under ‘AI drafts’ in Settings.

Is what I say stored anywhere?

No. It is turned into text on the device and discarded once it fills the field or is closed. It is never sent to a server.

Does the AI decide the amount?

No. Amounts you speak are found by rules and shown to you; if there are several you pick one. Check the item and amount yourself before the customer signs.

Does the summary become part of the report's document fingerprint?

No. If you add it to the completion report it is printed in its own section and is not part of the document fingerprint (hash).

Subscription

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I restore a subscription?

Tap ‘Restore purchases’ in the app's Settings or subscription screen, using the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payment, so request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
