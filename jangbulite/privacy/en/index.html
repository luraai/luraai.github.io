Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Jangbu Lite has no account and never sends photos, signatures, amounts or customer details to LuraAI servers. Every record stays on your device. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The AI draft features also run only on your device.

1. Information we collect

LuraAI ("we") does not collect personal information through Jangbu Lite ("the app").

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking frameworks and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app has no code that sends your job-site records to us.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device so the app can work.

  • Job site name, trade, contract amount, amount already received, customer pseudonym and warranty period you enter
  • Original progress photos with their capture time, and text read from material labels
  • Change-order items and amounts, and the signature image the customer draws with a finger
  • The hash chain linking the records, the digital signatures on them and timestamp responses
  • The signing key for the records. It lives in the device Keychain, is used only on this device and is not transferred to other devices.
  • App settings (glove mode, warranty reminder settings) and whether a subscription is active

Whether app data is included in a full device backup (iCloud Backup, Finder backup) follows your device settings, and Apple manages that backup.

When you use "Speak it", your speech is turned into text on the device and is not stored. The text is discarded once it fills the field or is closed, and it never enters the record.

3. When the app uses the network

The app does not send photos, signatures, amounts, job-site names or customer details to any server. It uses the network only in these cases.

  • Timestamps: when you seal a record, only its SHA-256 hash (fingerprint) is sent to an RFC 3161 timestamp authority (currently freetsa.org and DigiCert) to obtain proof that it existed before that time. A hash is a number that cannot be turned back into the original; no original files or job details are sent. When offline, the request waits until you are connected. The authority's own privacy policy applies to the request (for example your IP address), and we do not receive it.
  • Subscription payments: buying and restoring a subscription is handled by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details.
  • Speech-recognition language asset: the first time you use "Speak it", Apple's system may download the Korean speech-recognition asset. Apple handles that download; we receive nothing, and nothing you say is transmitted.
  • Info links: tapping a link in the app (legal notice, privacy policy, terms) opens that address in your browser.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only when you take a progress photo. Reading material-label text runs on the device and the photo does not leave it for recognition.
  • Notifications: requested only if you turn on warranty reminders. They are scheduled on the device and do not go through a server.
  • Microphone: used only while you use "Speak it". If you decline, typing works as before.

If you decline a permission, only that feature is unavailable and the rest keeps working.

5. AI draft features

For subscribers on Apple Intelligence devices using the app in Korean, the app offers speaking a change order, title ideas, drafting a summary and customer message, and natural-language search. These features run only on Apple Intelligence's on-device model and on-device rules, and your input, photos and amounts never leave the device. We do not use company servers, external AI services or Private Cloud Compute.

  • AI text can be wrong, so it appears as a dashed draft and does not enter any field or record until you confirm and fill it in.
  • Amounts and counts use only values calculated from your records; the AI never decides an amount. Check the item and amount yourself before the customer signs.
  • If you put the summary in a completion report it is printed in its own section and is not part of the document fingerprint (hash).
  • The app gives no legal judgment. You can turn AI drafts off any time under ‘AI drafts’ in Settings.

6. Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app receives job-site name, trade, balance due, warranty end date, change-order count, part of the latest hash and seal alerts from the paired iPhone through Apple's system connection only. Nothing can be created or changed from the Watch, and no server is involved.

7. Export and sharing

Invoice and completion-report PDFs and backup files leave the device only when you send them yourself from the share sheet. They can contain job-site names, amounts, customer signatures and photos, so check the recipient and the app you share with. We have no involvement with a file once it is handed over.

8. Customer information

Before recording a customer's name, signature or contact details you must have that customer's consent, and we recommend using a pseudonym. You are responsible for complying with applicable law for the information you enter.

9. Disclosure to third parties

We do not collect your information, so there is nothing for us to sell or share with third parties.

10. Deleting data

Deleting the app removes the job-site records stored on this device, and we cannot recover them. If you need to keep them, save a file with ‘Export backup’ in Settings before deleting. Subscription records are managed by Apple, and you can cancel in your subscription settings.

11. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes, and significant changes are announced in the app update notes.
