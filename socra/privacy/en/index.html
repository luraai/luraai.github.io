Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 October 2026 · 한국어

Socra has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your code, your conversations with the tutor and your progress stay on your device. We run no servers for this app, so we cannot see any of it.

1. What we collect

LuraAI (“we”) collects no personal information through Socra (“the app”).

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never ask for or collect a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and no code that sends data to LuraAI.

2. What is stored on your device

This data is created by you and stays on your device:

  • The code you write, your projects, your solved problems, attempts, hints used and streaks
  • Your mistake notebook: which tests failed, the kind of mistake, the example inputs and results you saw, and wrong guesses in “predict first”
  • Problems you write or import, and your progress on them
  • Your conversations with the tutor, kept per problem
  • App settings, and whether you bought Pro (confirmed with Apple)
  • Optional downloaded AI models

Deleting the app deletes this data from the device. Your code is never uploaded for execution: Python, JavaScript, SQL, Lua, Ruby and C all run inside the app.

3. The AI tutor

Hints and answers are produced on your device by Apple’s on-device model (Apple Intelligence, Foundation Models) or by an optional model you download in Settings. Your code, your questions and the tutor’s answers are not sent to LuraAI or to any cloud AI service. The tutor is designed not to hand out complete solutions.

4. Things that use the network

Only the following features use the internet or a local network, each because you choose to use it:

FeatureWhat happens
Downloading an AI model (Pro, optional)The app downloads the model file you pick from Hugging Face. Hugging Face sees an ordinary download request (such as your IP address) under its own policy. No code or conversation is sent.
GitHub sync (Pro, optional)If you sign in with GitHub, the project files you choose to push are sent to your own GitHub repository using GitHub’s API. The sign-in token is kept in the iOS/macOS Keychain. GitHub handles that data under its own policy; we never receive it.
iCloud progress sync (Pro, optional, off by default)If you turn it on in Settings, your progress (which problems you solved, attempts, hints used and practice days) is stored in your own iCloud account with Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so your devices signed in with the same Apple Account stay in sync. Your code, drafts, project files and tutor conversations are not included. Apple handles that data under its own policy; we never receive it.
Pair coding (Pro, optional)Nearby pairing connects two devices directly over the local network (Bonjour), and your shared code and chat messages go between those two devices only. SharePlay uses Apple’s Group Activities service. The AI navigator runs on your device.
PurchasesPro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple (StoreKit). We never receive payment details or your Apple Account information.
Importing and exporting your problemsYou pick a JSON file with the system file picker, or share one with the system share sheet. The file is read and written on your device. We never receive it.
Sending feedbackSettings › Send feedback opens your mail app with our address filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email; if you do, we receive your email address and what you wrote.
Apple WatchProgress and flashcards move between your iPhone and Apple Watch with Apple’s WatchConnectivity. No server is involved.

5. Device permissions

Local network is requested only when you use nearby pair coding. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. Declining a permission only disables the feature that needs it.

6. Sharing and selling

We collect no personal information, so we have nothing to share or sell. The app uses no advertising identifier (IDFA), no cookies and no cross-app tracking.

7. Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no ads, no public feeds and no user-generated content. The only chat is the private chat with the person you pair with in Pair coding, sent directly between your two devices.

8. Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything stored on the device. Progress synced with iCloud can be removed in the device settings under Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage. If you used GitHub sync, you can revoke the app’s access in your GitHub settings and delete the repository there. Downloaded models can also be removed in Settings. The mistake notebook can be cleared, and problems you wrote can be deleted, in Settings › Study tools.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes are mentioned in the app update notes.
