Getting started

What should I do first?

On first launch Memora offers "Choose starter decks": travel, languages, education, history, general knowledge, K-culture and more. Pick some and start studying, or tap "+" to add your own cards. You can get starter decks later from the Decks tab, and replay the onboarding from Settings.

Can I import from Anki or CSV?

In the Decks tab, choose Import and paste text (up to 1,000 characters at a time). Memora understands Anki's "Notes in Plain Text" export, CSV/TSV and "word - meaning" lines, including tag columns and cloze (fill-in-the-blank) notes. Check the column roles and review the list of look-alike cards the app would skip. Opening an Anki package (.apkg) file directly is not supported, but you can export your Memora cards as .apkg and open them in Anki. Importing is a Pro feature.

Can I make cards from photos or documents?

In the New card screen choose "Create from documents or photos" and add a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text or Markdown file, or a photo. Memora drafts key terms, cards and quizzes. You can take a photo with your device's camera app or pick one from your gallery. Everything happens on the device and only the cards you pick are saved. Free includes 3 a day; Pro is unlimited.

Studying

How do the rating buttons work?

Look at the card, tap "Show answer", then choose Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Each button shows the interval until the next review. If you tapped the wrong one, use "Undo last rating".

I finished today's study. Can I keep going?

Choose "Study again as practice" on a deck to go through its cards again without changing the review schedule. You can also study cards you pick in the card list.

Where do I take quizzes?

Use the Quiz tab to quiz yourself from a deck, your wrong-answer notes or cards you pick; missed questions collect in your wrong-answer notes. With Pro you can also quiz weak cards, tags, clusters, today's cards or all cards, and feed the results back into your review schedule.

Where can I see my study history?

Open the calendar in the Study tab for day-by-day history, and the Stats tab for trends over time. Free shows the last 30 days; Pro adds longer periods.

How many new cards a day?

As many as the deck's "New cards per day" setting (20 by default), minus the new cards you already started today. When you study several decks together, the total stays within the largest deck limit.

Can I get review reminders?

Turn them on in Settings under review reminders and pick a time. On Android 13 and later you need to allow the notification permission; if you decline, the rest of the app keeps working. Reminders are scheduled on your device only.

How does read-aloud work?

Read-aloud uses the voices (text-to-speech) installed on your device. If a voice for your language is missing, install its voice data in your device's text-to-speech settings.

Free and Pro

What is free and what is Pro?

There is no trial; the limits below apply from day one. Free: 3 decks, 50 cards of your own (starter-deck cards don't count), 1 quiz a day (10 questions), stats for the last 30 days, and 3 document/photo card drafts a day. Reviewing, studying again (practice mode), the study calendar and reminders are always free. Pro removes the limits and adds AI deck creation, the AI study guide, contrast cards, concept clusters, look-alike warnings, meaning search, importing and output language selection. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. Decks and cards you already have beyond the free limits are never deleted; you just can't add more.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app on a device signed in to Google Play with the Google account you bought with, and your purchase is recognized. If it does not appear, tap "Restore purchases" on the Pro screen. Your purchase belongs to your Google Play account, so it works on a new device with the same account.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play. See the refund information at Google Play Help.

AI features

AI deck creation isn't working.

Choose the model in Settings > AI model. We recommend Gemma 4 (about 2.6 GB), which is strong across languages. Gemma 4 and Qwen3 (about 1 GB or 2.7 GB) are downloaded once over Wi-Fi after you confirm the size and data-usage notice; after that everything runs on the device. If storage or memory is short, choose a smaller model. AI deck creation is a Pro feature.

Can I use the app without downloading a model?

Yes. Models are optional, and without them core features work with text-based analysis. AI deck creation needs a language model.

Are AI-made cards accurate?

Not always. Smaller models in particular can state wrong facts. Review cards before saving, and check medical, legal, tax and investment topics against official sources.

The model finished downloading but it's slow.

AI generation takes time depending on your device. Close other apps and keep Memora in the foreground while it works. The 4B model is recommended only on devices with plenty of memory.

How do I remove a downloaded model?

In Settings > AI model tap "Delete" on the model to free the storage. You can download it again whenever you like.

Backup and widget

How do I back up, and move to a new phone?

In the backup section of Settings tap "Back up now" and choose where to save the file. On the new phone use "Restore from file" on the same screen and open that file. Cards you already have are skipped; only missing ones are restored. If Android automatic backup is turned on for your device, app data may also be restored when you set up a new device (you can turn it on or off in your device settings).

My cards disappeared.

If you saved a backup file yourself, restore it from Settings with "Restore from file". If you can't find your cards, email us before uninstalling the app.

How do I add the home screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, open Widgets, and drag the Memora widget onto the screen. It shows how many cards are due today and your study streak.

Is there a Wear OS app?

Yes. Install Memora on your Wear OS watch from Google Play (the watch must be paired with the phone that has Memora). Today's cards and quizzes appear on the watch, your ratings are saved to the phone right away, and the phone keeps the schedule. A tile and watch-face complications show the cards left today. The watch app needs the phone and does not work on its own.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
