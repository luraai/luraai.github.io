Memora Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 October 2026

English 日本語 한국어 简体中文 繁體中文

Memora (Android) has no accounts and sends none of your cards or study history to LuraAI. Embeddings, AI deck creation and document analysis all run on your device. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK.

1. What we collect

LuraAI ("we") collects no personal information through Memora (the "app").

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never ask for or collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking frameworks, ship no third-party SDKs, and do not use the advertising ID.
  • The app contains no code that sends your cards or study history to us.

2. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is needed for the app to work:

  • Decks, cards, review history (when you reviewed and how you rated), and analysis results such as clusters and confusable pairs
  • Embeddings (numeric vectors) used to compare the meaning of cards. They are computed on the device.
  • Text read from documents or photos when you create cards from them, quizzes you generate, and your quiz history and wrong-answer notes (kept on the device only)
  • App settings (theme, fonts, read-aloud voices, review reminder time and so on) and your Pro entitlement
  • The number of cards due today and your study streak, used to display the home screen widget (used only on the device)
  • Backup files you save with the app's file backup feature to a location you choose. You keep and manage those files; they are never sent to us.

3. Device backup (Android Auto Backup)

If Android's automatic backup is turned on for your device, the app's data may be stored in your own Google account backup and restored when you set up a new device. This backup is a feature provided by Android and Google, and you can turn it on or off in your device settings. We cannot access this backup data. The app's file backup and restore is separate: it lets you create and load a backup file at a location you choose.

4. When the app uses the network

The app never sends your cards to a server. It uses the network only for:

  • Downloading model files: the embedding model for meaning analysis (about 165 MB) and the AI deck model you choose (Gemma 4 about 2.6 GB, Qwen3 about 1 GB or 2.7 GB) from Hugging Face. Before any download the app shows the size and a data-usage notice, and it starts only after you confirm (Settings > AI model). This is an ordinary file request; no cards or study content are transmitted. Hugging Face's privacy policy applies to that request (for example your IP address). You can delete downloaded models in Settings at any time.
  • Purchases: the lifetime purchase is handled by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Whether you have purchased is confirmed through Google Play Billing.

Core features such as reviewing and quizzes work without an internet connection. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.

Wear OS watch (optional): if you install the watch app, today's cards, quizzes and your ratings are exchanged only between your phone and your paired watch through Google's Wearable Data Layer (Google Play services). Memora does not send this data to any server of ours.

The app also checks whether you are on mobile data (network state permission) so it can warn you before a large model download.

5. AI features

Finding duplicate and confusable cards, meaning search, creating cards and quizzes from documents and creating decks with AI all run on your device, using downloaded models (EmbeddingGemma, Gemma 4, Qwen3). The topics and documents you enter never leave your device. Without a model, the app falls back to text-based analysis. AI-made cards can be wrong, so please review them before saving, and check medical, legal, tax and investment topics against official sources.

6. Device permissions

  • Internet: used only to download model files and to confirm purchases.
  • Notifications: requested only if you turn on review reminders (optional); they are scheduled on the device and do not go through any server.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): used for Google Play purchases.
  • Camera: the app does not request the camera permission. When you photograph a textbook page, it opens your device's camera app; the photo is used on the device to read text and is not sent to a server.
  • Photos and files: only the photos and documents you choose in the system picker are read.
  • Read-aloud uses the voices (text-to-speech) on your device and does not use the microphone. The app does not use location, contacts or microphone permissions.

If you decline a permission, only that feature is unavailable; the rest of the app keeps working.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not collect your information, so there is nothing for us to sell or share with third parties. The model download (Hugging Face) and purchase (Google Play) described above are separate requests governed by those providers' own terms and policies.

8. Deleting your data

Your data exists only on your device, so you can delete it as follows:

  • In the app's Settings, Delete all data deletes this device's decks, cards, review history, documents and quizzes.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the app data on the device.
  • A copy kept in Android automatic backup can be removed in your device settings (Google account backup management), and a backup file you saved yourself is removed by deleting that file.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the effective date above changes; significant changes are announced in the app update notes.
