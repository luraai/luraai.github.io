PageFleet PDF

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

PageFleet PDF does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no backend server.

Documents

Documents, edits, scans and AI results are processed and stored on your device. We cannot receive or see them.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, your documents and folders are also kept in your own iCloud account through Apple’s iCloud service so that your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account can use them. They go only to your iCloud, are not sent to us, and we cannot access them. Turning it off stops syncing; what is already in your iCloud can be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Passwords of protected documents are never synced.

AI

AI features run on Apple Intelligence, on your device. They use no network and send nothing anywhere. There is no downloadable AI model.

Web pages

The “Web page → PDF” tool loads only the single address you type in, directly from your device, exactly like opening it in a browser; that website sees your IP address. Nothing else is sent.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive payment details. Purchase status and the free daily counter are stored only in your device’s Keychain.

Permissions

  • Camera: document scanning
  • Photos: converting photos to PDF, saving page images
  • Face ID: unlocking the app

Declining a permission does not affect other features.
