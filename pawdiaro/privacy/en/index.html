Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 October 2026

Pawdiaro has no accounts and does not send your diary, photos or care records to LuraAI servers. Your records stay on your device, and the app uses no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

1. What we collect

LuraAI (“we”) collects no personal information through Pawdiaro (the “app”).

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never ask for or collect a name, email, phone number or location.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking frameworks and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app has no feature that sends data to our servers.

2. What is stored on your device

The following is stored on your device so the app can work. We cannot see it.

  • Pet profiles (name, species, birthday or adoption date, photo, tag colour)
  • Diary entries (text, photos, mood, author name) and notebooks
  • Care tasks and completion history (vaccines, parasite care, medication, checkups and so on), weight records and habitat temperature/humidity readings
  • Settings (reminder time, units and so on) and your subscription status

When a photo is saved, its metadata including GPS location is removed and the image is resized.

3. Notifications

Care reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Their content does not pass through any server. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings.

4. AI features

Diary drafts, monthly recaps and schedule text recognition are processed on your device. On supported devices they use Apple’s on-device model (Foundation Models) and Vision; the text and photos you provide never leave the device. We do not use any AI server of our own or of a third party.

Optional: on devices without Apple Intelligence you can choose to download a small open-source model under ‘AI models’. Only a request to download the model file from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) is made; none of your records or photos are sent. The download starts only after you agree.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by the Apple App Store. The app only checks your purchase status through StoreKit and receives no card or Apple ID details.

6. Sharing and export

Only when you tap ‘Share’ or ‘Save to Files’ does the app create a diary PDF, a vet visit summary PDF or a year-in-review image and hand it to the iOS share sheet. Files are created on your device, and you decide where they go.

7. Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Watch

Siri/Shortcuts answers and the Apple Watch ‘today’s care’ list are built from records already on your device. Siri’s voice processing follows Apple’s policies.

8. Deleting your data

Use Family tab › Delete everything to remove your records and settings at once; deleting the app also removes its data from the device. If your device uses iCloud Backup, app data may be included in that backup according to your Apple ID settings. You can also save a copy with ‘Export data’.

9. Children

The app does not collect personal information from children. No feature asks for an age or personal details.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the effective date on this page and note important changes in the app update notes. Contact: contact@luraai.io
