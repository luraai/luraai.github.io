FAQ

Where are my records stored?

Everything is stored only on your device. There is no developer server and no account. Before changing devices, save a copy with ‘Export data’ in the Family tab.

What is the difference between Free and Plus?

Free includes 1 pet, 1 diary, 5 care tasks per pet, 6 months of weight history and 5 AI drafts a month. Plus lifts those limits and adds the diary PDF and the vet visit summary PDF. Choose monthly, yearly (14-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions and choose Pawdiaro. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can also use ‘Restore purchases’ in the app.

Where do I log habitat temperature and humidity?

Pick a small pet, bird, reptile, insect or aquatic pet and open the Weight tab to find the ‘Habitat’ card. It only records numbers and never judges them.

How do I make a summary for my vet?

In the Family tab choose ‘Vet visit summary PDF’ (Plus), pick a pet and create the PDF. Vaccine, parasite care, medication and checkup history plus recent weights fit on one page. Pawdiaro is for records and reference only; please consult your vet for any medical judgment.

What about a pet that has passed away?

Open Our pets, tap the pet and turn on ‘Crossed the Rainbow Bridge’. Reminders for that pet stop, and the days together and every record stay in the diary.

What can I do with Siri?

Ask “Pawdiaro, what’s my next care task?” or “Pawdiaro, what’s on today?”, or open the diary and weight screens. The same actions are in the Shortcuts app.

I am not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on in Settings › Notifications › Pawdiaro and that the reminder time in the app is right. Completed tasks and tasks of a pet in memorial mode are not announced.
