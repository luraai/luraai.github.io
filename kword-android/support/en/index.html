Help for kWord, a private Korean vocabulary app for Android. If your question is not answered here, write to us at contact@luraai.io.

Frequently asked questions

How does scheduling work?

kWord uses FSRS-5 spaced repetition. After each answer you grade how well you knew the word (four grades). The scheduler predicts when you are about to forget it and brings it back at that moment. One adaptive daily session chooses the exercise type from how well you remember each word, and it ends when the day's reviews are done. The retention report in Records shows how well the predictions matched your real results.

What is free and what is Pro?

The free tier includes 300 items: 25 words from each TOPIK level and 10 sentences from each scenario category. kWord Pro is a single one-time purchase that unlocks everything else and the extras. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

How do I restore my purchase?

Connect to the internet, sign in to the same Google account you used to buy kWord Pro, open the kWord Pro screen and choose Restore purchase. Your purchase belongs to your Google account, so reinstalling the app or using a new device restores it. If it does not, write to us with your Google Play order number.

How do I back up and restore my progress?

In Settings, export your progress to a JSON file and import it on another device. If Android Auto Backup is turned on, Android may also restore the app's data when you set up a new device with the same Google account. Backups are stored in your own Google account; we never receive them.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for kWord in Android Settings (required on Android 13 and later) and that reminders are switched on in the app. Then set the battery usage of kWord to Unrestricted or remove it from battery optimisation: some manufacturers stop background work to save power. Reminders are not exact alarms and may arrive a few minutes late. Do Not Disturb can also silence them.

The Korean voice is missing or silent

Check the media volume. Then open Android Settings, find Text-to-speech output, and make sure the speech engine (for example Speech Services by Google) has Korean voice data installed. Install or update the Korean voice there. The exact menu names depend on your device.

Pronunciation scoring does not work

Scoring is optional and needs the microphone permission, which the app asks for when you start it. It uses your device's speech recognition, and an offline Korean language pack may need to be installed in the speech settings of your device. The app tells you when it cannot guarantee on-device recognition. Shadow mode works without a microphone.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is available in Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic (right-to-left) and Hindi. The app follows your device language; on Android 13 and later you can also set it per app in Android Settings.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings and choose Reset learning data, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy of your data.
