Overview

kWord ("the App") is a Korean vocabulary learning app for Android published by Kang Seok Ju (Luraai). It has no accounts and no servers of ours. This policy explains exactly where your data goes.

What we collect

We collect nothing. There is no sign-up, login or account. We never ask for or receive your name, email address, phone number or location. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDK, and no code that sends your data to a server we operate.

What is stored on your device

Everything the App produces is stored locally on your device:

  • Per-word progress, review scheduling and your answers
  • Study sessions, streaks, level and daily goals
  • Settings such as display language, theme, text size, daily limits and reminder times
  • Words you favourite or add yourself
  • Local usage statistics that power the statistics screen; they are never transmitted
  • Pronunciation results: the word you practised, the text your speech was recognised as, and the outcome. Your recording itself is never stored.

Backup and your data

If Android Auto Backup (Google backup) is turned on for your device, Android may copy the App's data to the backup in your own Google Account. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google's terms and your device settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings.

The App can also export your progress to a JSON file and import it again. That file goes wherever you choose to save or share it; the App never uploads it.

Notifications

Study reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We run no push server, and no notification content is sent anywhere. The App asks for notification permission only when you choose to use reminders, and works fully without it.

Purchases

kWord Pro is a one-time in-app purchase sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and receipt validation; we never receive or store your payment details or Google Account information. The App checks your purchase with Google Play on your device. There is no subscription.

Text-to-speech

Spoken Korean uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your Android device. The App sends no text to any server of ours. Depending on the engine, Android or the engine's provider may download voice data to your device under their own terms.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only if you choose scored pronunciation practice or voice study. The RECORD_AUDIO permission is requested at that moment, and the App works without it. Shadow mode needs no microphone.

The App uses Android's built-in speech recognition (SpeechRecognizer) and asks it to recognise speech offline, on your device, whenever possible. Android does not always allow offline recognition to be enforced. Depending on your device, speech recognition may be performed by the speech service of your device manufacturer or of Google, and your audio may then be processed by that provider under its own privacy policy. When the App cannot guarantee that recognition stays on your device, it tells you.

kWord itself does not record, store or collect your audio. The audio is handled by the system's speech service for a single attempt only. The recognised text and your result are kept on your device.

Network use

Studying does not need an internet connection. The App uses network access for Google Play Billing and so that Android's speech and text-to-speech services can check or download voice data. The App sends no learning data to us.

Deleting your data

To delete the data on your device, open Settings in the App and choose Reset learning data, or uninstall the App. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete.

A device backup can be managed or deleted in your Android backup settings and in your Google Account. Exported JSON files can be deleted from the place where you saved them.

Sharing and selling

We collect no personal information, so there is none to share with or sell to anyone. The App does not use the advertising ID.

Children's privacy

kWord is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, and it has no social features or user-generated content shared with others.

Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: study reminders (optional)
  • RECORD_AUDIO: scored pronunciation practice and voice study only (optional)
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Google Play Billing and system speech or text-to-speech checks

The App does not request location, contacts, camera or storage permissions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any change will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
