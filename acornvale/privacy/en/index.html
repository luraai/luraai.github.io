Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Acornvale is an offline game with no accounts and no company servers. Your progress is stored on your device and the company collects no personal information directly. When you watch a rewarded ad, the Google ads SDK may process information as described below.

1. Information we collect directly

LuraAI (“we”) does not directly collect any personal information through Acornvale (“the app”) — no name, email or phone number. There is no sign-up, no communication with servers we operate, and no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Game progress (acorns, levels, gear, forest, tree rings) and settings are stored on your device.
  • If iCloud key-value storage is on, a small summary (total spring seeds, best stage and a few numbers) syncs to your iCloud account. Apple processes it; we cannot access it.
  • The adventure log uses Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence) only on supported devices to phrase text. Everything stays on the device and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings.

3. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The app only shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch (no interstitial or banner ads). It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may process:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), device information, IP address and ad interaction data
  • Purposes: serving ads, measuring performance and preventing fraud. Google is responsible for this processing under the Google Privacy Policy.

Before the first ad, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You may decline and play exactly the same (you will see non-personalized ads). In the EEA, UK and similar regions, a Google User Messaging Platform consent form appears first, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings > Ad privacy settings. Valley Pass subscribers and Auto Collect owners get ad rewards without watching ads.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Manage, cancel or request refunds in your Apple account settings or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

5. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, leaderboard scores (best stage, hibernation count) and achievements are sent to Apple Game Center and handled under Apple's privacy policy. The game works the same if you are not signed in.

6. Notifications

Notifications are off by default. If you turn them on, the app schedules a single local notification on your device for when offline storage is full. We do not use server push.

7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Age and consent settings apply to the ad SDK.

8. Deleting data

“Reset all data” in Settings clears progress on the device, and deleting the app removes all data stored on it. The iCloud summary can be deleted under Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will post the new date here. Contact: contact@luraai.io
