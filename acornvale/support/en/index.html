Support

Acornvale help · Contact: contact@luraai.io

How do I restore purchases?

Open More > Shop and tap “Restore purchases” at the bottom. With the same Apple Account your Valley Pass and one-time purchases come back.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone go to Settings > (your name) > Subscriptions > Acornvale. For refunds visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

My buried acorns disappeared

If you stay away long, buried acorns can be “forgotten” and grow into oaks — that is a game rule, not a bug. Check your oaks and permanent bonuses on the Valley tab. Add a marker to a plot if you never want it forgotten.

Offline rewards look small

Free: 40% efficiency, up to 8 hours. Valley Pass: 55%, up to 12 hours. Changing the device clock safely limits rewards.

My progress is gone

The app restores from its latest backup automatically. On a new device the iCloud summary (spring seeds, best stage) is restored. If it still looks wrong, email us your device model and iOS version.

No ad is available

Check your connection and the daily limit (8 per day). In regions that require it, you can change ad privacy choices in Settings.
