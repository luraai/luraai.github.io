Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026

Coinjot has no account and does not send your records to any LuraAI server. Receipts and voice are processed on your device only, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Sync between your own devices goes through your own iCloud only.

1. Information we collect

LuraAI ("we") does not collect personal information through Coinjot (the "app").

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We use no analytics, tracking or advertising frameworks and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app contains no network code that sends your records to our servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device only.

  • Expense and income records (amount, merchant, memo, date, category), ledgers, categories, monthly budget and recurring expenses
  • Receipt images (a thumbnail, the original, or nothing, depending on the option you choose)
  • App settings (theme, app lock, reminders, profile name and the badge you pick)
  • Achievement badges and the badge images Image Playground creates on this device

3. Receipt, voice and AI processing

Receipt text recognition (Vision), speech recognition (Speech) and AI extraction, spending comments and summaries (Apple Intelligence on-device models) all run on your device; photos and audio are never uploaded. Audio is not saved to a file and is discarded right after recognition. Anything read by AI is shown on a confirmation screen with its source text and is saved only after you confirm.

AI spending comments and the spending pattern report put facts computed on your device into sentences for reference only; they are not financial or tax advice.

4. iCloud sync

If iCloud is available, Coinjot syncs your records between your own devices (such as iPhone and iPad) using Apple's CloudKit private database in your own iCloud account. There is no Coinjot server. Amounts, merchants and memos are stored in encrypted fields and we cannot access or read them. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud processing.

5. Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app exchanges data only between your iPhone and your Apple Watch (Apple's WatchConnectivity). Records entered on the watch are saved after you confirm them on iPhone.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only when you photograph a receipt to read its text.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only to turn a spoken expense into text; audio is not stored.
  • Face ID: used only to unlock the app when app lock is on; biometric data is never given to the app.
  • Notifications: used only for the optional month-end report reminder.

7. In-app purchases

Lifetime access (one-time purchase) and monthly or yearly subscriptions are sold through Apple's StoreKit. Payment and receipt validation are handled entirely by Apple; we receive no payment or Apple account information. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

8. Sharing and selling

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share with or sell to third parties. We use no cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking.

When you export a monthly report as a PDF (Pro), the PDF is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail, Messages); Coinjot does not upload it anywhere.

9. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Deleting data

Settings › Delete all data removes records on your device and in iCloud, and deleting the app removes data on the device. Data left in iCloud can be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

If you use several ledgers (Pro), deleting a ledger deletes its records and receipt photos on your device and from your iCloud copy.

11. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and important changes are announced in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@luraai.io
