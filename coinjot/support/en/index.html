Getting started

Can I try it without a receipt?

On the Ledger tab tap Scan, then "Try a sample receipt". The extracted amount appears on the confirmation screen, and sliding to confirm saves it.

What if the AI reads a value wrong?

You can edit the amount, merchant and category on the confirmation screen, and nothing is saved until you confirm.

Does it work on devices without Apple Intelligence?

Yes. Text recognition suggests amount candidates, and manual entry always works.

Apple Watch

Is a record saved right away when I enter it on the watch?

No. What you say or write on the watch is sent to your iPhone and shows up under "From your Watch" at the top of the Ledger tab, where you confirm it before it is saved.

The watch does not show this month's summary.

Open Coinjot on your iPhone once and the summary is sent to the watch. Also make sure the watch app is installed.

Subscriptions and purchases

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free: records, reports, the Mosaic theme and 15 AI entries a month. Pro: unlimited AI entry, AI spending comments, the spending pattern report, achievement badges and profile, partner sharing invitations, widgets, Pro themes and CSV export. Choose lifetime access (one-time purchase) or a monthly or yearly subscription; prices vary by region.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions; Pro stays active until the paid period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com

How do I restore purchases?

Tap "Restore" on the Pro screen in Settings. You need to use the same Apple Account.

Sharing and data

How do I share a ledger with my partner?

Send an invitation from the Shared tab and you both record through iCloud. The person you invite does not need Pro. You need two different Apple Accounts, both signed in to iCloud.

Where is my data?

Only on your devices and in your own iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use another currency?

A new ledger uses your device region's currency. Changing currency or converting with exchange rates is not available yet.

I found a bug or want to suggest a feature.
