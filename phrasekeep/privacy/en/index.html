Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

Phrasekeep has no account and no server. The sentences you capture, your cards and your review history are processed and stored only on your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Information we collect

None. LuraAI (“we”) does not collect personal information through Phrasekeep (“the app”).
  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Sentences, cards, review history and settings are stored only on your device. We do not operate a server that receives them.

2. Camera and photos

The app uses the camera and your photos to read text from books, signs and menus. Text recognition runs on your device and the pictures never leave it. Original photos are not saved by default. If you turn that setting on, they are kept on this device only and are not synced.

3. Translation

Sentences are translated with Apple’s Translation feature. Apple downloads language packs to your device and translation runs on the device. Downloading a language pack uses the network.

4. AI drafts and the known-word lens

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, meaning and example drafts are written by Apple’s on-device language model, and you always confirm a draft before it is kept. You can also choose to download a small language model. The download starts only after you agree, and the files come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which can see your IP address. After the download the model runs only on your device. The known-word lens compares your cards with the text on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

5. Notifications, widgets and export

Review reminders are scheduled locally; there is no push server. The home screen widget reads the same on-device data. CSV/TSV export creates a file on your device and you choose where to share it. Source sentences are exported only as an excerpt, for copyright reasons.

6. Purchases

Phrasekeep Pro (monthly and yearly subscriptions and a lifetime purchase) is sold through the App Store. Apple processes payment and the app never receives your payment details. The app checks your entitlements on the device through StoreKit.

7. iCloud sync (optional)

iCloud sync is available with Pro and is off by default. If you turn it on, your cards and review history are stored in your own private iCloud storage through Apple’s CloudKit. Apple keeps this data and we cannot access it.

8. Selling and sharing

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share or sell, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

9. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Deletion

In Settings you can delete all data. Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device. If you used iCloud sync, records in your private iCloud storage are removed when you delete all data in the app.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and significant changes are noted in the app’s update notes.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@luraai.io
