Using Phrasekeep

How do I bring in a sentence?

Tap the capture button on Home and choose camera, photos or paste. On supported devices you can also use the live camera and tap the text on screen to pick it.

What is the known-word lens?

It sorts the words in a captured sentence by your own cards. Bold underline is a new word, dotted is a word you are learning, and plain text is a word you know. Each sentence gets a summary such as “Known words 6/9 · 3 new”, and you can turn it off in Settings. The more cards you keep, the more words you recognize when you meet the same text again.

Translation doesn’t appear.

On-device translation needs a language pack. If a prompt appears, download it, or choose “Continue without translation” and write the meaning yourself. After the pack is installed, translation works in airplane mode.

I don’t see AI drafts.

You need a supported device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it you can still make cards from the translation suggestion or by typing. You can also download a small model in Settings.

How does review work?

FSRS-based spaced repetition decides which cards are due today. You can change the target retention and the number of new cards per day in Settings.

Phrasekeep Pro

What is included in Pro?

Unlimited cards, captures and AI drafts, iCloud sync, skins, the widget and CSV/TSV export. Choose monthly, yearly (7-day free trial) or lifetime. The free plan includes 50 cards, 5 captures a day and 3 AI drafts a day.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore, open the purchase screen and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I lose my cards if the subscription ends?

No. Your cards and history stay. Only the Pro features go back to the free limits.

Data and privacy

Where is my data?

Only on your device (and in your private iCloud if you turn on sync). Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I export or delete my data?

Create a CSV/TSV file from Export in Settings, and use “Delete all data” to remove your cards and history.

What about copyright?

Phrasekeep is built for personal study. Original photos are not saved by default and exports include only an excerpt of each source sentence. Please don’t use it to store or share whole books or textbooks.
