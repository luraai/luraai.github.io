Framesift Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer: Kang Seok Ju (luraai) · Contact: contact@luraai.io

English 한국어

Framesift (Android app and Wear OS app) helps you sort the photos and videos on your device by date, type, scene and screenshot content, find similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos, and clean them up. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Summary

  • Your photos never leave your phone. There is no account, no server and no third-party analytics or advertising.
  • The app does not collect or share personal data.

What the app reads

  • Photos and videos on your device (with your permission): the app reads thumbnails and basic information (date, size, resolution, whether a photo is a favorite or a screenshot) to group similar photos, find blurry photos, list large videos and sort photos by date, time of day, type and album. On Android 14 and later you can allow access to only the photos you select.
  • On-device image analysis: the app analyzes thumbnails to recognize the scene (for example people, food, nature, documents) and, for screenshots only, reads the text and barcodes in the image to sort screenshots into receipts, tickets, verification codes, chats, maps, QR codes and account info and to suggest outdated ones. This uses Google ML Kit models that are bundled inside the app, so the images and the recognized text stay on your phone. The recognized text itself is not saved.
  • The original photo files are not copied, uploaded or stored by the app.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Analysis results only: numbers such as a blur score, a small feature vector used to compare photos, group membership, a scene label, a screenshot kind (and a date printed on a ticket, if any), the photo type, and your decisions (keep / candidate). No photos, no thumbnails and no recognized text are stored.
  • Settings (similarity level, blur sensitivity, protection options, appearance).
  • A daily counter used for the free daily limit.
  • Cleanup history (how many photos you removed and their estimated size) shown in Storage insights. You can clear it there.
  • You can erase analysis data in Settings > Reset analysis data. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored.

Creating albums

If you use "Create album", the app asks Android for your approval and then moves the selected photos into a new folder in your gallery. Nothing is copied, uploaded or deleted.

Removing photos

Photos are removed only after you confirm in the app and in Android's own confirmation. On Android 11 and later they move to the trash and can be restored for about 30 days. On earlier Android versions there is no trash and removal is permanent; the app tells you before you confirm. Copies kept by Google Photos or another backup service are managed by that service.

Purchases

Pro (yearly subscription or lifetime purchase) is processed by Google Play. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the product. We do not receive your payment details.

Wear OS

If you use the Wear OS app, the phone sends it a small summary through Google's Wearable Data Layer: the number of candidates, their estimated size, the number of analyzed photos, the last analysis time and whether Pro is active. No photos, thumbnails or file names are sent. The data stays between your own devices.

Network

The app has no server. Network access is used by Google Play services for purchases and the watch connection. The app blocks cleartext traffic. Google ML Kit runs on the device with bundled models; your photos and text are not sent for recognition. As with other Google libraries, ML Kit may send technical diagnostics (such as performance and crash information) that do not contain your photos or text; see Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Children

Framesift is not directed to children.

Your choices

You can revoke photo access at any time in Android settings, switch to "selected photos only", reset analysis data, or uninstall the app. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no data to request or delete from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date.
