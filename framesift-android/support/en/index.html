Getting started

  1. Open Framesift and tap Allow photo access. On Android 14 and later you can choose "Select photos and videos". No photos on the device? Tap Explore with sample photos.
  2. Wait for the first analysis. It works on thumbnails and continues where it stopped if you leave the app.
  3. Review similar photos, screenshots and blurry photos. Swipe left to mark a candidate, right to keep.
  4. Open the Candidates tab and move them to the trash. Android asks for a final confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded? No. Analysis and results stay on your phone.
Did swiping delete anything? No. Swiping only marks candidates. Photos are removed only after you confirm.
Can I get a removed photo back? On Android 11 and later, open the Trash in your gallery app (for example Google Photos > Library > Trash) within about 30 days. On earlier versions there is no trash.
Why are some photos missing? If you chose "Select photos only", only those are analyzed. Use Settings > Manage selected photos or change access in Android settings.
Why are results different from another app? Similarity depends on the similarity level (Settings > Similarity level) and the blur sensitivity slider. Estimated sizes are approximate.
The analysis is slow. Battery saver and a warm phone slow it down on purpose. Charging helps.
Subscription and purchases. Pro is a yearly subscription (free trial when offered) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Use Settings > Restore purchases after reinstalling or changing phones. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.
Wear OS. The watch app shows the candidate count and estimated size. Open Framesift on the phone once so the data syncs; clean-up always happens on the phone.
Reset. Settings > Reset analysis data erases the app's analysis results and starts over. Your photos are not affected.

Report a problem

Email contact@luraai.io with your phone model, Android version, app version (Settings > Version) and what happened. Do not send photos.
