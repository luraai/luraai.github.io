Deskdown Privacy Policy — Android

Effective: 2026-10-16 · Developer: LuraAI (Kang Seok Ju) · Contact: contact@luraai.io

English 한국어

Summary

Deskdown has no accounts, servers, ads or tracking. Everything you record stays on your device, and the developer does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app does not talk to any server of the developer or of third parties. Only the optional study room (direct device-to-device connection on your Wi-Fi) and Google Play billing use the network.

What is stored on your device

  • Study sessions: start/end time, study time, subject, lift (violation) count, whether a hardcore reset happened
  • Noise summaries: place name, average dB, threshold-crossing count (the sound itself is never stored)
  • Wrong-answer note text, settings (exam, goals, alerts, theme), your custom exams and edited exam schedules
  • Coach tips (sentences computed on the device)

Records are kept for up to 5 years; older records are deleted when the app starts. Use Settings > "Delete all records" at any time; uninstalling the app removes everything. If Android Auto Backup is on, this data may be included in your own Google account backup, which the developer cannot access.

A photo is opened only when you pick one with the system photo picker; text is read on the device by a recognizer bundled in the app and the photo is not saved. The app does not use location, contacts, camera or storage permissions.

Study room (optional)

When you create a room with people on the same Wi-Fi, only a nickname, the current status (focused, lifted, ...) and counted time are exchanged, encrypted with the room code. The code is not advertised and no developer server is involved. Nothing is sent after you leave the room.

Wear OS watch app (optional)

If you install the watch app, your phone's current study state (timer, today's study time, D-day, one coach line) is passed between your phone and watch through the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play services. It is not sent to any developer server.

Purchases (Deskdown Pro)

Deskdown Pro is processed by Google Play billing. Google handles payment information; the developer receives none. Whether you own Pro is checked on the device from your Google Play purchases.

Third parties, ads and tracking

None. No analytics, advertising ID or external servers. The app includes Google Play Billing, Google ML Kit text recognition (offline model bundled) and the Wearable Data Layer library; none of them sends data to the developer.

Children and teenagers

The main users are students preparing for exams. The app does not ask for age or collect personal data and avoids pressure framing and random rewards. Purchases follow Google Play's parental approval settings.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, this page and the in-app "Privacy policy" are updated. Contact: contact@luraai.io
