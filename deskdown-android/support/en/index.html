FAQ

The timer does not start when I lay the phone down.

The device needs a gravity sensor, and the screen must face down for at least 0.6 s. A slight tilt (within about 37 degrees, e.g. on a book) still counts. If no sensor signal arrives for 10 s, a "start manually" button appears.

Do I have to keep the app open?

Yes, while studying. Leaving the app or locking the screen counts as lifting the phone. The screen dims automatically. A "counting study time" notification is shown while measuring and disappears when you end the session.

Hardcore mode reset my record.

If the phone is not put back within the grace period (5-30 s in Settings) after a lift, today's in-app record is reset. Mock-exam records are kept. Hardcore is off by default.

Notifications do not arrive.

Allow them in Android Settings > Apps > Deskdown > Notifications. Battery optimization may delay background cues (in-app cues are exact).

Why the microphone? Is audio recorded?

Only to compute the sound level (a dB number) in the noise meter. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent.

The study room will not connect.

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi (some public networks block device-to-device traffic) and use the same 4-digit code. Creating a room needs Deskdown Pro; joining is free.

How do I restore Deskdown Pro?

Settings > Deskdown Pro > Restore purchase, signed in with the Google account used to buy it. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

How do I delete my data?

Settings > Delete all records. Uninstalling the app also removes everything (Auto Backup copies are managed in your Google account settings).

An exam date or schedule is wrong.

Dates and timetables change every year and the data is unofficial reference material. Edit the date and periods in Settings > Exam (Pro) and check the official announcement. Please report errors to contact@luraai.io.

The Wear OS app says the connection was lost.

The phone and watch must be connected and Deskdown must be running on the phone. The watch app mirrors the phone session; starting and stopping happen on the phone.
