Getting started

Add your child's profile, then pick a built-in story or tell the moon about today. Tap Play to read aloud; use the timer to fade out at bedtime.

AI stories are unavailable on my device

AI stories need a device that supports on-device AI (Gemini Nano through AICore) with the model downloaded, or an AI model file you import with Pro (Settings › AI models). Built-in stories, narration and the library work on every device.

Restore or cancel a purchase

Settings › Restore purchases. To cancel a subscription, open Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Move stories to another phone

Settings › Export a backup file, then Import a backup file on the other device. Android Auto Backup may also restore your data when you set up a new phone.

Report a problem story

Open the story, tap ⋯ › Report a problem. The story is hidden immediately; you can also email us.

Delete my data

Settings › Delete all data.
