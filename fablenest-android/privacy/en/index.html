Fablenest Privacy Policy (Android)

In short

Fablenest does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your child's nickname, age band, interests and topics to avoid; the stories you create or read (text); and app settings. This data is kept only in the app's private storage and is never sent to our servers. If you use Android Auto Backup, Google may back up this data under Google's own terms. You can also export a backup file yourself in Settings.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only while you tap the microphone or moon button to dictate. The app does not record or store audio. Speech is converted to text by the speech recognition service on your device: on-device when your device supports it, otherwise the speech service selected in your device settings (which may process audio on its provider's servers under the provider's terms).

AI story writing

Stories are written on your device by Gemini Nano (Android AICore) or, with Pro, by an AI model file you import yourself. Your child's details and prompts are not sent to our servers. Downloading the Gemini Nano model is handled by Google AICore. Generated stories are checked by a built-in safety filter and you can report or hide any story in the app.

Narration

Narration uses the text-to-speech voices installed on your device. Voices that require a network connection are not used.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns whether a purchase is active.

Notifications, widget and Wear OS

The optional bedtime reminder is scheduled on your device and does not include your child's name. The home-screen widget shows no personal data. If you use the Wear OS app, story titles and the sentence being read are sent between your phone and watch through Google Play services; this data does not pass through our servers.

Children

Fablenest is for parents and guardians and is not designed for children to use alone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The nickname you enter is stored only on your device.

Reporting content

If you report a story, it is hidden on your device right away. If you choose to send an email, you are sending it yourself and we receive only what you send.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data removes profiles, stories, settings and imported model files from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes them.
