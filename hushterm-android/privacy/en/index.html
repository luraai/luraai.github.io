Hushterm Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · Publisher: Kang Seok Ju (luraai.io) · Contact: contact@luraai.io

English 한국어

1. Summary

Hushterm is an SSH and SFTP client. It has no accounts, no developer server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Hosts (name, address, port, user name, notes, folders, tags), port forwards, snippets, command history and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Passwords and private keys are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. Keys created with "hardware-backed key" never leave the secure hardware.
  • Terminal output is kept in memory while a session is open and is not written to disk by the app.
  • The agent history (what a coding agent asked and what you decided, newest 200 entries), the last five server-check results per host and check, the checks you wrote yourself, and which practice lessons you finished are stored in the app's private storage on your device only. They are not synced and are excluded from backups. Server checks send read-only commands to the server you chose and keep only a short summary of the findings, never the full output.

3. Where data goes

  • Your servers. When you connect, Hushterm sends your credentials and the commands you type to the server you chose, over SSH. That traffic goes only between your device and your server.
  • Google Play. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Wear OS. If you use the watch app, host names, snippet titles and pending approval requests are sent between your phone and your watch over the Wearable Data Layer. They do not pass through our servers.
  • Backups. Android Auto Backup may back up the host list, port forwards, snippets and settings to your Google account. Passwords, private keys, purchase caches, agent history, check results and practice progress are excluded. You can also export a backup file yourself.

4. AI features

Command suggestions, error analysis, summaries (including summaries of what an agent did and plain-language readings of server checks), connection diagnosis and voice input run on your device: with the on-device model provided by Android (AICore) where available, or with a built-in rule engine. Hushterm contains no third-party AI SDK, no AI API key and no server, and it sends no prompt, terminal output or audio off your device. Voice input uses the system's on-device speech recognition only, and only after you grant the microphone permission and tap the microphone button. Hushterm detects permission prompts and the working state of coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or aider inside your own SSH session; it never contacts those services. The Linux practice course runs on a simulated server inside the app and uses no network.

5. Permissions

Internet (to reach your servers), notifications (approval requests, finished or failed agent alerts and the background-session notice), a foreground service (to keep open sessions connected), microphone (voice input, optional), biometrics (app lock and protected keys, optional).

6. Children

Hushterm is a developer tool and is not directed to children.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stores on the device. Because we hold no data, there is nothing to delete on our side. Backups in your Google account can be removed in Google settings.

8. Changes and contact

We will post any change on this page. Questions: contact@luraai.io.
