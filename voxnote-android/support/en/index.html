VoxNote is an Android app for meeting notes, voice recording and transcription. It records audio, shows a live transcript, and then creates a title, short summary, key decisions and action items. Everything is processed on your phone, with no account and no VoxNote server.

Quick start

  1. Open VoxNote and allow microphone access (and notifications, so you can control recording from the notification).
  2. Choose a template (Dev scrum, Sales meeting, Interview or General) and tap Record.
  3. Speak or run your meeting. The live transcript appears as you go. Add bookmarks for key moments.
  4. Tap Stop. VoxNote creates the note with a summary, decisions and action items.
  5. Review the note, check off action items, rename speakers, then export or share it.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no transcript, or why am I asked to install a language pack?

VoxNote uses the speech recognition installed on your phone, and "On-device recognition only" is on by default. Install the offline language pack for your language in your phone's speech settings (for example Google speech services), then try again. VoxNote does not silently use the network.

File transcription is not available.

Transcribing imported audio and video files requires Android 13 or later. Supported formats are MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, MP4 and MOV, up to 3 hours.

What are the free limits?

Free use includes one recording per day (up to 60 minutes) and one file transcription per day. Limits reset at your local midnight. VoxNote Pro, an optional subscription, removes these limits.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Google Play, go to Subscriptions, select VoxNote and cancel. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play, not inside the app.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings and use "Delete all notes and audio". Individual notes can be deleted from the notes list. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

How do I export my notes?

Open a note and use the export or share options (Markdown, plain text, PDF, SRT, VTT, JSON backup, audio file). You can also export all notes as a ZIP and import a JSON backup later.

Recording stops or is cut off in the background.

VoxNote records with a foreground service and shows a notification while recording. Keep notifications allowed, and if your phone stops the app, exclude VoxNote from battery optimization in the system settings.

How accurate are transcripts and summaries?

Recognition quality depends on your phone's speech service, the installed language packs, background noise and microphone distance. Summaries and action items are created automatically by an on-device, rule-based summarizer, work best in Korean and English, and can contain errors. Please check important notes.
