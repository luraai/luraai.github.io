VoxNote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16

Deutsch English Español Français 日本語 한국어 Português 中文

Summary: VoxNote has no server, and we do not collect your data. Your recordings, transcripts and notes stay on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Who we are

VoxNote (Android package com.luraai.quillvox) is a meeting notes, voice recorder and transcription app developed by Luraai (Kang Seok Ju).

Contact: contact@luraai.io

2. What is processed on your device

VoxNote works entirely on your phone. The following is created and stored on the device only:

  • Audio recordings and any audio files you import
  • Transcripts (with timestamps and speaker labels)
  • Summaries, key decisions and action items (including detected owners and due dates)
  • Bookmarks, tags, pins and your app settings

We do not receive any of this. The app has no account, no login and no VoxNote server.

3. What leaves your device

VoxNote itself uploads nothing. The only cases in which data is handled outside the app are the following:

  • Google Play subscription payments. If you subscribe to VoxNote Pro, Google Play handles the purchase and your payment information. The app does not see your card details.
  • System speech service (only if you choose). "On-device recognition only" is on by default. If you turn it off, your phone's system speech service may process audio the way that service normally works, under its own terms. This is outside VoxNote's control.
  • Android Auto Backup. If backup is enabled on your phone, Android may back up the notes database (audio files are excluded) to your own Google account. We cannot access this backup.
  • Calendar entries. If you grant calendar access and tap the add button on an action item, the entry is written to the calendar you choose on your phone. Nothing is written without your tap.
  • Items you share or export. Files you export or share go where you send them, through the apps you select.

5. Ads, analytics, tracking and children

VoxNote contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking technology, and does not build advertising profiles. The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where your local law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect any personal information.

6. Your controls

  • Delete all notes and audio at any time with "Delete all notes and audio" in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone.
  • You can revoke the microphone, notification and calendar permissions in your phone's system settings.
  • You can export your notes (Markdown, plain text, PDF, SRT, VTT, JSON backup, audio, or all notes as a ZIP).
  • You can cancel a subscription anytime in Google Play under Subscriptions.

7. Data retention

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. We hold no copies. Backups made by Android Auto Backup are managed through your Google account settings.

8. Security

Because your notes are processed and stored on the device and are not sent to us, there is no VoxNote server that could leak them. Their protection also depends on your device, so we recommend a screen lock and regular system updates. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open your notes.

9. Third parties

  • Google Play (Billing): handles subscriptions and payments under Google's terms.
  • System speech service and text-to-speech engines: these belong to your device's vendor (for example, Google speech services) and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. VoxNote does not control them.

10. Recording consent

You are responsible for using VoxNote lawfully. Laws on recording conversations differ by country and region. Please obtain the consent of the people you record and follow the rules that apply to you. Speech recognition and automatic summaries can contain errors, so please verify anything important.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Wear OS app

When you record on the watch, the audio file is passed to your paired phone over the Wear OS device-to-device connection (the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play services) and processed there. No developer server is involved; the watch app uses the microphone permission and a foreground service only for recording, and keeps the file on the watch until the phone confirms it has it.
