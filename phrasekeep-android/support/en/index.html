FAQ

How do I bring in sentences?

Tap the shutter on the home screen to use the camera, pick a photo or paste text. You can also select text in another app and choose "Keep in Phrasekeep", or share to Phrasekeep from the share sheet.

Translation doesn't work.

The first time you use a language pair the app downloads a translation language pack (about 30 MB). Connect to the internet and tap "Try again". You can also continue without a translation and type it yourself.

I don't get AI drafts.

On-device AI (Gemini Nano) works only on supported phones. In Settings > AI model you can download a small model, or continue with the translation suggestion and manual entry. AI drafts can be wrong, so always check them.

Japanese readings (furigana) are empty.

Readings for Japanese kanji can't be generated on the device, so you type them (open a card > Reading). Chinese pinyin is filled in automatically.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check that reminders are on in Settings and that notifications are allowed in Android settings. On phones with aggressive battery saving a reminder can be a few minutes late.

I got a new phone. Can I move my cards?

Create a backup file in Settings > Create backup file and use "Restore from backup file" on the new phone. If Android auto backup is on it may also restore when you install the app.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Play Store > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Phrasekeep Pro (Settings > Manage subscription opens it). Your cards and history stay when a subscription ends. Refunds follow Google Play policy.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. Remove it with Settings > Delete all data. See the privacy policy: https://luraai.io/phrasekeep-android/privacy/en/
