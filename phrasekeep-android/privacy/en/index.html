Phrasekeep Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026 · Operated by LuraAI · Contact: contact@luraai.io

English 한국어

Phrasekeep (the "app") has no server and no accounts. What you create with the app (sentences read from photos, cards, decks, review history, settings) stays on your device only and is never sent to LuraAI.

1. What we collect

LuraAI does not collect, store, sell or share personal information through this app. The app uses no ads, no advertising ID and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. What is processed on your device

DataHow it is handled
Camera and photosText is recognized on the device (Google ML Kit, on-device). Original photos are not saved by default; only if you turn on "Keep original images" are they stored in the app's private storage. They are not included in backups or exports.
Sentences, cards, decks, review historyStored in the app's private database on your device. Delete everything with Settings > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
TranslationUses Google ML Kit on-device translation. The sentences you translate never leave the device.
AI drafts (optional)Generated on the device by the system model (Gemini Nano via Google AICore) on supported phones, or by a small model you choose to download (Qwen2.5). Your text is not sent to a server. Results are labelled "AI draft" and only become final after you confirm them.
NotificationsReview reminders are scheduled on the device only.
WidgetThe home screen widget shows a summary stored on the device.

3. When the app uses the network

  • Translation language packs: downloaded from Google the first time you use a language pair (your sentences are not sent).
  • AI model (optional): model files are downloaded from Hugging Face only if you agree. Standard connection data such as your IP address is visible to that service; your text and photos are not sent.
  • Subscriptions: payments are handled by Google Play. The app only checks whether a subscription is active and never receives your payment details.
  • All connections use HTTPS.

4. Third-party services

Google Play services (billing), Google ML Kit (text recognition, translation, AI access) and MediaPipe (running the optional model). These may process diagnostic information under their own policies; see Google's Privacy Policy and the ML Kit data disclosure.

5. Backups

If Android auto backup is on, cards and settings may be included in your Google account backup (original photos and downloaded models are excluded). Google handles that backup. You can also create a backup file yourself in Settings > Create backup file.

6. Copyright and personal study

The app is designed to keep sentences from books, signs, menus and subtitles for your own study. Exports apply an excerpt limit (30 source sentences per deck, 200 characters each, 100 for CJK). Please do not share other people's copyrighted works.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

8. Your rights and deletion

Because we hold no personal data about you, no deletion request is needed. Use "Delete all data" in the app or uninstall it to remove everything. Questions: contact@luraai.io.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and mention it in the app's release notes.
