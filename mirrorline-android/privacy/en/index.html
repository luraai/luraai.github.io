Mirrorline - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16 - Publisher: Kang Seok Ju (luraai) - contact@luraai.io

English 한국어

Mirrorline is a wellness app for the left-right balance of your face and for posture habits. This policy explains what the Android app does with your data. The short version: your face never leaves your phone.

What the app does with your camera

  • When you start a check, the app uses the front or back camera. A model that is part of the app (Google MediaPipe) finds face points in each camera picture on your phone.
  • Camera pictures are analysed in memory and are never saved, uploaded or shared. The face points are used for one measurement and then discarded. The app does not recognise or identify anyone and does not create a face template.
  • If your shoulders are in the picture, the app also reads two shoulder points the same way, for an optional reference reading.

What is stored on your phone

  • The numbers that come out of each check (for example "eye line tilt 1.2 degrees"), the pattern name, your baseline, your plan and routine progress, your age range (never a birth date), your answers to the quick questions, and the version of the consent you agreed to.
  • This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfer, so it stays on this phone only.
  • You can delete it at any time: Me > Delete all face data removes measurements, baseline and plan; Me > Reset everything also removes your age range, answers and consent. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

What is not collected

  • No account, no name, no email address, no phone number, no location, no contacts, no photos from your gallery, no microphone.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking, no data sale or sharing. The app does not ask for the INTERNET permission, so it does not communicate with any server of its own.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details and purchase history under its own privacy policy; the app only learns whether Mirrorline Pro is active and keeps that yes/no on the phone so Pro works offline.

Children

Mirrorline is not for children. The app asks for an age range and cannot be used by people under 14.

Not a medical product

Mirrorline measures the skin surface in a flat picture and describes patterns. It does not diagnose, screen or treat any condition and cannot tell whether a difference comes from bone, muscle or soft tissue. If you notice sudden changes, pain or trouble opening your mouth, talk to a doctor or specialist.

Your rights

Because the data never leaves your phone, you control it directly in the app. If you have a question about this policy, write to contact@luraai.io.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we update this page and the in-app consent, and the app asks for your agreement again.
