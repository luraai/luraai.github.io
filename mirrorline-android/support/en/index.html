Common questions

The check never starts. Hold the phone level (the Level gauge), look straight at the camera, keep your face relaxed with your mouth closed, and stay inside the distance range (front camera about 30-60 cm, back camera about 35 cm-1.8 m). Use even light from the front. Remove glasses with strong reflections.

Why are the numbers called "reference estimates"? Mirrorline reads a flat camera picture. The size of your face is estimated from the width of your iris, so numbers can be off by a few percent. Take three checks under the same conditions to see your own margin of error.

Why does the app not say "left" or "right"? The direction of left/right is still being confirmed on real devices, so the app says "one side" until then.

Front and back camera results do not match. They are kept apart on purpose and never compared.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Me > Delete all face data removes measurements; Me > Reset everything returns the app to its first start. See the privacy policy for details.

Subscriptions. Manage or cancel in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases on the Mirrorline Pro screen after reinstalling or changing phones. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

Medical questions. Mirrorline is a wellness app and cannot give medical advice. If you notice sudden changes, pain or trouble opening your mouth, talk to a doctor or specialist.
