FAQ and how to reach us

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All analysis and search, including reading text in screenshots and finding photos with words, happens only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. The one exception is the AI model file that a Pro user chooses to download, and even then no photos or searches are sent.

How do I find photos with words?

Tap the “Find photos with words” field at the top of Home. Free users search with scene, type and date words such as “screenshots”, “videos”, “food” or “last year food”. Pro users can type a full sentence like “kid with an umbrella on a rainy day”. Results are a guide, and you can only add them to Candidates. Nothing is deleted right away.

What is the AI model and why download it?

Sentence search in Pro needs an AI model that compares photos and words (Google EmbeddingGemma 2, about 388 MB). It is downloaded only when you tap Download in Settings > AI Model; Wi-Fi is recommended. Only the model file is received, and no photos or searches are sent. Free users never download it. After the download the app needs time to read your photos with the screen on, and you can search the newest photos first.

How do I remove the model and search index?

Tap Settings > AI Model > Delete model. The downloaded model and the photo index are removed and your photos are not affected. You can download it again later.

How do I delete photos safely?

Mark photos as Candidates (one by one, in a group, or with the batch buttons). Nothing is deleted until you open the Candidates tab and confirm. iOS then asks once more and moves them to Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for about 30 days in the Photos app.

What does “Outdated screenshots” mean?

Screenshots that were probably used already: verification codes older than 7 days, directions older than 30 days, and tickets or bookings whose date has passed. They are only suggestions. Review them and choose yourself.

How do I create an album?

Open any group under Browse by category and tap the Create album button at the top right. Photos are added to the new album in Photos; they are not copied, moved or deleted.

Some photos were skipped

Photos that exist only in iCloud are not downloaded for analysis and are skipped. If you gave access to selected photos only, add more under the app’s Settings > Manage selected photos. The first launch after an update may analyze your library again.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free includes word search and lets you move up to 30 photos a day to Recently Deleted. Framesift Pro (yearly with a 7-day free trial, or lifetime) adds Find photos with words (AI model download), removes the daily limit and unlocks Large Videos. There are no weekly plans.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Restore: app Settings > Restore purchases. Cancel or manage: app Settings > Manage subscription, or iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
