FAQ and how to reach us

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All analysis and search, including reading text in photos and Easy Search, happens only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and the app uses the internet only for purchases through Apple.

How does Easy Search work?

Type words or a sentence in the search field at the top of the Home tab and tap search, for example “receipt”, “dog”, “sea”, “starbucks”, “last year food” or “last month screenshots”. It searches scenes and objects in photos, text written in photos and screenshots, screenshot types and dates, and it is free. Results are a guide, and you can only add them to Candidates. Nothing is deleted right away.

The photo I want doesn’t show up

Photos are searchable once they have been analyzed. A big library takes a while, so check the progress at the top of the Home tab. If your phrase is long, try the key words (for example dog, sea, receipt). After an update the app analyzes your photos once more to store labels and text. Long-press the refresh button on the Home tab to analyze everything again from scratch.

Where is the text it reads stored?

Only in the app’s storage on this iPhone, for search. Text from verification-code and account screenshots is not kept, and runs of six or more digits are removed before saving. You can erase it in Settings > Reset analysis data, and deleting the app removes everything.

How do I delete photos safely?

Mark photos as Candidates (one by one, in a group, or with the batch buttons). Nothing is deleted until you open the Candidates tab and confirm. iOS then asks once more and moves them to Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for about 30 days in the Photos app.

What does “Outdated screenshots” mean?

Screenshots that were probably used already: verification codes older than 7 days, directions older than 30 days, and tickets or bookings whose date has passed. They are only suggestions. Review them and choose yourself.

How do I create an album?

Open any group under Browse by category and tap the Create album button at the top right. Photos are added to the new album in Photos; they are not copied, moved or deleted.

Some photos were skipped

Photos that exist only in iCloud are not downloaded for analysis and are skipped. If you gave access to selected photos only, add more under the app’s Settings > Manage selected photos. The first launch after an update may analyze your library again (to store labels and text for search).

What is free and what is Pro?

Most features, including Easy Search, are free, and you can move up to 30 photos a day to Recently Deleted. Framesift Pro (yearly with a 7-day free trial, or lifetime) removes the daily limit and unlocks Large Videos. There are no weekly plans.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Restore: app Settings > Restore purchases. Cancel or manage: app Settings > Manage subscription, or iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
