Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Framesift analyzes your photos only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Your photos and any text read from screenshots never leave your device, and we collect no personal information.

1. Information we collect

LuraAI (“we”) does not collect, transmit or share any personal information through Framesift (“the app”). The app has no sign-up, does not talk to any server we operate, and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDK.

2. How the app uses your photos

  • Photo library access is used to find similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos, to group photos by date, type, album and scene, and to move photos you confirm to Recently Deleted or add them to an album you create.
  • On-device analysis only. Apple’s Vision framework runs on your iPhone to compute image features, sharpness and scene categories, and to read text and barcodes in screenshots so they can be sorted into receipts, tickets, codes and so on.
  • Text read from screenshots is not saved. Only a category (for example “receipt”) and, if present, a date used to judge whether a screenshot is outdated are kept.
  • Photos are never copied, uploaded or shared. Photos you do not confirm are never changed or deleted. Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album, where they stay for about 30 days.

3. Information stored on your device

The app keeps analysis results (image features, sharpness score, scene and screenshot category), your Keep/Candidate choices, settings and cleanup totals in its own storage on your device. It contains no photo files or thumbnails. You can erase analysis data in the app’s Settings > Reset analysis data, and deleting the app removes everything.

4. Apple Watch

The companion Watch app shows numbers only (candidate count, estimated size, last scan time) sent from your iPhone. No photos or photo identifiers are sent.

5. Purchases

Framesift Pro (yearly subscription or lifetime purchase) is processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, and request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will post the new date here. Contact: contact@luraai.io
