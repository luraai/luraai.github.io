Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Framesift analyzes your photos, and searches them with words, only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Your photos, your searches and any text read from screenshots never leave your device, and we collect no personal information. If you are a Pro user and choose to, the app downloads an AI model file once (section 3).

1. Information we collect

LuraAI (“we”) does not collect, transmit or share any personal information through Framesift (“the app”). The app has no sign-up, we operate no server, and it contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDK. The app uses the internet only for purchases through Apple and for the AI model download that a Pro user starts (section 3).

2. How the app uses your photos

  • Photo library access is used to find similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos, to group photos by date, type, album and scene, and to move photos you confirm to Recently Deleted or add them to an album you create.
  • On-device analysis only. Apple’s Vision framework runs on your iPhone to compute image features, sharpness and scene categories, and to read text and barcodes in screenshots so they can be sorted into receipts, tickets, codes and so on.
  • Text read from screenshots is not saved. Only a category (for example “receipt”) and, if present, a date used to judge whether a screenshot is outdated are kept.
  • Photos are never copied, uploaded or shared. Photos you do not confirm are never changed or deleted. Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album, where they stay for about 30 days.

3. Finding photos with words and the AI model

  • Free: word search by scene, type and date (for example “screenshots” or “last year food”) only uses information the app already analyzed on your device. It needs no internet, and free users never download the AI model.
  • Pro: when you start it yourself in Settings > AI Model, the app downloads, once, the AI model used to search with full sentences (Google EmbeddingGemma 2, about 388 MB). The model is a data file and the app only receives it. Your photos, searches and device identifiers are not sent.
  • The model file is served from GitHub (GitHub Releases). As with any web download, connection details such as your IP address may appear in GitHub’s access logs. We do not receive or use those logs, and GitHub’s privacy policy applies to them.
  • After the download everything runs on your device. The app feeds small versions of your photos to the model and stores the resulting lists of numbers (the search index) on your device; your search words are turned into numbers on the device and compared there. The search index contains no photo files or images.
  • You can delete the model and the search index any time in Settings > AI Model. Both are excluded from device backups and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Search results are a guide. Nothing is deleted from the results: you add photos to Candidates and confirm in the Candidates tray.

4. Information stored on your device

The app keeps analysis results (image features, sharpness score, scene and screenshot category), your Keep/Candidate choices, settings and cleanup totals in its own storage on your device. If you downloaded the model in Pro, the model file and the search index are on your device too. It contains no photo files or thumbnails. You can erase analysis data in the app’s Settings > Reset analysis data, and deleting the app removes everything.

5. Apple Watch

The companion Watch app shows numbers only (candidate count, estimated size, last scan time) sent from your iPhone. No photos or photo identifiers are sent.

6. Purchases

Framesift Pro (yearly subscription or lifetime purchase) is processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, and request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will post the new date here. Contact: contact@luraai.io
