Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Framesift analyzes your photos, and finds them with words, only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Your photos, your searches and any text read from photos never leave your device, and we collect no personal information. The app uses the internet only for purchases through Apple.

1. Information we collect

LuraAI (“we”) does not collect, transmit or share any personal information through Framesift (“the app”). The app has no sign-up, we operate no server, and it contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDK. The app uses the internet only for purchases through Apple.

2. How the app uses your photos

  • Photo library access is used to find similar photos, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos, to group photos by date, type, album and scene, to find photos with words or a sentence, and to move photos you confirm to Recently Deleted or add them to an album you create.
  • On-device analysis only. Apple’s Vision framework runs on your iPhone to compute image features, sharpness and scene and object labels, and to read text and barcodes in screenshots and in photos that look like documents, signs or receipts.
  • Text that is read is kept on your device for search only. It lives in the app’s own storage on your device and never leaves it. Text from screenshots classified as verification codes or account details is not kept, runs of six or more digits (cards, phone numbers and the like) are removed before saving, and text is not read from photos that look like passports, cards or license plates.
  • Photos are never copied, uploaded or shared. Photos you do not confirm are never changed or deleted. Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album, where they stay for about 30 days.

3. Easy Search

  • You find photos with words or a sentence such as “receipt”, “dog”, “starbucks” or “last year food”. The app compares what you type with the scene and object labels, the text it read, the screenshot type and the date that it stored on your device.
  • It is free, and nothing extra, such as an AI model, is downloaded. It needs no internet, and your searches never leave your device.
  • Search results are a guide. Nothing is deleted from the results: you add photos to Candidates and confirm in the Candidates tray.

4. Information stored on your device

The app keeps analysis results (image features, sharpness score, scene and screenshot category, scene and object labels, and the text read for search), your Keep/Candidate choices, settings and cleanup totals in its own storage on your device. It contains no photo files or thumbnails. You can erase analysis data, including labels and text, in the app’s Settings > Reset analysis data, and deleting the app removes everything.

5. Apple Watch

The companion Watch app shows numbers only (candidate count, estimated size, last scan time) sent from your iPhone. No photos or photo identifiers are sent.

6. Purchases

Framesift Pro (yearly subscription or lifetime purchase) is processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, and request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will post the new date here. Contact: contact@luraai.io
