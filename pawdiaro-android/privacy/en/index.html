Pawdiaro Privacy Policy (Android)

Effective: 2026-10-16 · Operator: Luraai (Kang Seok Ju) · Contact: contact@luraai.io

English 한국어

Summary

Pawdiaro has no developer server, no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your diary, photos, pets, schedules and weights are stored only on your phone. The operator does not collect or receive this data.

What the app handles

DataWherePurpose
Pet profiles (name, kind, birth month, colours, photo), diary text, mood, photos, care schedules and completion log, weights, your name (optional)App-private storage on this deviceProvide the app's features
Settings (reminder time, unit, phrase chips on/off)This deviceKeep your preferences
Subscription / purchase stateHandled by Google Play; the app only reads the entitlementUnlock Plus features

None of this is sent to a server or shared with third parties by the operator. There are no analytics, advertising or crash-collection SDKs.

Photos

  • Photos come from the system photo picker or the camera app. The app asks for no storage or camera permission.
  • When a photo is saved it is re-encoded, so location (GPS) and other metadata are not kept.

Notifications

Care reminders are planned and shown on the device. No server push is used. The notification permission (Android 13+) is requested when you save your first schedule.

Backup and export

  • If Google backup is turned on in your device settings, Google may back up the app's database and settings (not photos) to your own Google account. That is a Google service governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • "Export data" creates a ZIP of your diary, photos and records; you decide where it goes. "Import data" loads such a file on another device.

Wear OS watch

With the watch app, today's care list (pet names, schedule titles) travels between your own phone and your own watch through the Google Wearable Data Layer. It does not pass through any operator server.

Payments

Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details; the operator never receives card data. You can cancel a subscription in Google Play at any time.

Retention and deletion

  • All data lives on this device. Use Family tab > "Delete everything" to erase all records and photos, or uninstall the app.
  • Copies in Google backup are managed in your Google account settings.

Children

Pawdiaro is intended for people aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect children's data. It does not collect any user data at all.

Medical notice

Pawdiaro is for records and reference only. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe or predict illness. If something worries you, talk to your vet.

Changes

Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: contact@luraai.io.
