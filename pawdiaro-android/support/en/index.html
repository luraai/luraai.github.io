FAQ

How do I start? Tap "+" in the Family tab to add a pet, then use the ribbon button in the middle of the tab bar to write today's entry.

Reminders don't arrive. Check Settings > Apps > Pawdiaro > Notifications. Reminders fire 7 days before, 1 day before and on the day at your chosen time. Completed tasks and tasks of a pet in memorial mode are not announced. In battery-saving modes they can be a few minutes late (the app does not use the exact-alarm permission).

Is photo location saved? No. Location and other metadata are removed when a photo is saved.

I got a new phone. Family tab > "Export data" creates a ZIP; on the new device use "Import data". Google backup/transfer also carries entries, schedules and weights, but move photos with the ZIP.

How do I cancel or restore a subscription? Cancel: Google Play Store > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Pawdiaro. Restore: "Restore purchases" on the Plus screen.

What is the difference between Free and Plus? Free includes 1 pet, 1 diary, 5 care tasks per pet and 6 months of weight history. Plus lifts those limits and adds the diary PDF and the vet visit summary PDF. Choose monthly, yearly (14-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Where do I log habitat temperature and humidity? Pick a small pet, bird, reptile, insect or aquatic pet in the Weight tab and scroll to the "Habitat" card. It only records numbers and never judges them.

How do I make a summary for my vet? Family tab > "Vet visit summary PDF" (Plus): pick a pet and create the PDF. Vaccine, parasite care, medication and checkup history plus recent weights fit on one page. Pawdiaro is for records and reference only; please consult your vet for any medical judgment.

What about a pet that has passed away? Open Family tab > Our pets, tap the pet and turn on "Crossed the Rainbow Bridge". Reminders for that pet stop, and the days together and every record stay in the diary.

What is the year in review? Timeline > ribbon icon > "Year in review" builds a card with a photo collage, a monthly chart and totals from your own entries, and you can share it as an image.

What are the app shortcuts and widgets? Long-press the app icon for "Next care", "Care today", "Write a diary entry" and "Log weight". Home-screen widgets show today's care, the next care with a D-day count, and your latest photo.

What about the watch app? On a Wear OS watch you can see and check off today's care. Changes made on the phone refresh the watch list.

Is this medical advice? No. Pawdiaro is for records and reference only. If you're worried about your pet's health, talk to your vet.
