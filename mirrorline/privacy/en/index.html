Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 October 2026 · 한국어

Mirrorline has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics. Your face is read on your iPhone and is never uploaded. Camera images, depth data and the face mesh are used only while a check runs and are not saved. Only the resulting numbers stay on your device.

1. What we collect

LuraAI (“we”) collects no personal information through Mirrorline (the “app”).

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We never receive your name, email, phone number, location, device identifier, face data or usage history.
  • We use no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs and ship no third-party SDKs.
  • The app has no feature that sends your measurements or any camera data to us or to anyone else.

2. What the app does with your face

To measure left–right balance, the app reads your face with the front TrueDepth camera while you take a check (a 3D face mesh, a depth map and expression values), or with the rear camera (2D facial landmark points found on the live image). This happens in the iPhone’s memory only. When the check ends, that data is discarded.

  • Not stored: photos, video, depth maps and face meshes.
  • Not used to identify you, to sign you in, or for advertising.
  • Not sent anywhere. Processing is entirely on the device.

3. What is stored on your device

The following stays on your device, in the app’s private storage, and is needed for the app to work:

  • The numbers from each check (for example eye-line tilt in degrees, chin position in millimetres), the pattern type, and your personal baseline and margin of error
  • Your plan and which routines you completed, the habits and any pain you log
  • Your settings: an age range (never a birth date), optional height, your answers to the short intake questions, and a record that you gave consent

This storage is protected by the iPhone’s built-in data protection and cannot be read while the device is locked. Nothing in it is shared with us.

4. Device permissions

Declining a permission only disables the feature that needs it.

PermissionWhy
CameraReading your face during a check: the front TrueDepth camera, or the rear camera as a reference-only alternative. Camera images are not saved or sent.
MotionChecking that your phone is level before a check. The readings are not stored.

The app does not use your location, contacts, photo library, microphone or health data.

5. Summaries and coaching

The plain-language summary of a report and your routine plan are produced on the device from fixed templates and a reviewed exercise library. No text or measurement is sent to any AI service.

6. Purchases

The app is free to download. Mirrorline Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, through Apple’s App Store. Payment, renewal and receipt validation are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive payment details or your Apple Account information. You can restore purchases from the Pro screen. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

7. Sharing, ads and tracking

We collect no personal information, so there is none to share or sell. The app uses no cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking.

8. This website

The app can open this privacy policy and the support page in your browser. These pages are static and served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request). We do not use analytics or cookies on these pages.

9. Children

Mirrorline is for people aged 14 and over. At first launch the app asks only for an age range, never a birth date, and does not let anyone under 14 take a check. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app collects none from anyone.

10. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Me › Delete all face data removes every check, your baseline and your plan from the device immediately and cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything else the app stored.
  • Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

11. Wellness, not medical

Mirrorline is a wellness app. It measures the skin surface and describes patterns; it is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or screen for any condition. It cannot tell whether a difference comes from bone, muscle or soft tissue. If you notice sudden changes, pain or trouble opening your mouth, talk to a doctor or specialist.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and material changes are announced in the app’s update notes.
