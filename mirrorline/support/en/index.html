Taking a check

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Face ID (the TrueDepth camera) gives the most detailed readings; the rear camera works on any supported iPhone, with estimated, reference-only numbers.

How do I take a good check?

Hold the phone level, 30–40 cm from your face, looking straight at the camera with a relaxed face and your mouth gently closed. Take off glasses and keep hair off your forehead. The four gauges (level, yaw, pitch, distance) light up when you are in range, and the app collects the frames by itself. Try to check at the same time of day, for example within an hour of waking.

The check will not start or keeps pausing.

Collection only runs while every gauge is in range. Follow the hint under the guide (move closer or farther, face forward, hold level, relax your face). Bright, even light helps; strong side light or a window behind you does not.

The camera is unavailable.

Allow camera access in Settings › Mirrorline, then try again.

Understanding your results

What does “no change” mean?

Three checks under the same conditions show how much your readings wobble on their own (your margin of error). Differences and changes smaller than that are shown as “no change”, so you only see changes that are real. Until you have three checks, the margins are provisional.

What are the patterns (Tilt, Chin-shift, and so on)?

They are plain descriptions of what was measured, such as eye and mouth lines tilting the same way. They are not scores, grades or conditions.

Can Mirrorline tell if something is wrong with my bones or jaw?

No. It measures the skin surface only and cannot tell whether a difference comes from bone, muscle or soft tissue. It also gives no diagnosis. If a difference worries you, or you notice sudden changes, pain or trouble opening your mouth, talk to a doctor, dentist or specialist.

Why do my numbers differ slightly between checks?

Lighting, head position and expression all move the numbers a little. That is why the app uses gauges, a median over many frames, and your own margin of error.

Plans and habits

What does the evidence label on a routine mean?

Each routine shows how strong the research behind it is (moderate, low–moderate or low). Techniques with no supporting evidence are left out, and the app tells you which.

Something hurts during a routine.

Stop. Open the routine and tap “This hurts” to remove similar routines from your plan.

Mirrorline Pro and purchases

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: your first three baseline checks, then one check a month, your main pattern, a two-week plan and your last two sessions in the chart. Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime): every pattern, the surface map, plans beyond two weeks, unlimited checks and your full history.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Me › Mirrorline Pro and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Mirrorline Pro › Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com

Your data

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. Camera images, depth data and the face mesh are not saved at all; only the resulting numbers are. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Me › Delete all face data removes every check, your baseline and your plan. Deleting the app removes everything else.
