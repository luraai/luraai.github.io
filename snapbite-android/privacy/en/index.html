SnapBite Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16
Operator: Kang Seok Ju (luraai) - contact@luraai.io
Applies to: SnapBite for Android and its Wear OS companion app

In short

  • SnapBite has no account and no server of its own. The developer does not collect, receive or store your photos, videos, location or any other personal data.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Photos, videos and everything the app learns about your preferences stay on your device.

What the app processes, and where

SnapBite processes the following only on your device, to provide the features you use:

  • Camera frames. The camera preview and the photos and videos you take are processed on the device to apply filters and manual controls. Filters are rendered on the phone's GPU.
  • Audio. Only while you record a video with sound, so that the sound is saved in the video.
  • Photos you pick. When you open a photo in the editor, you choose it with the Android system Photo Picker. SnapBite only receives the photos you select.
  • Editing and AI Edit. Adjustments, filters, erasing objects, removing the background of people and painting styles run on the device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • On-device AI helpers. Photo tags (image labeling with Google ML Kit), filing a photo into a folder by tag, picking a best shot in a burst, recognizing text in a photo, and detecting QR codes all run on the device with ML Kit models. Tags are written into the photo file's description field; you can switch this off in Settings.
  • Suggestions (Pro). An on-device recommender learns which looks and settings you use and offers a single suggestion. The learned data stays in the app's private storage and nothing is applied automatically.
  • Location (optional). Location tagging is off by default. Only if you turn it on, SnapBite asks for location permission and writes the coordinates into the EXIF data of your own photos on the device. The coordinates are not transmitted anywhere by SnapBite.
  • App settings. Your preferences (for example grid, timer and language) are stored locally in the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not send your photos, videos, audio, location or settings to any server.
  • We do not show ads, use an advertising ID, or include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Saving and sharing

Photos you save are written to your gallery using Android's MediaStore, in Pictures/SnapBite, and videos in Movies/SnapBite. SnapBite does not request storage or media-read permission. When you share a photo, location and other metadata are removed by default; you can change this in Settings. What you share and with whom is your choice and is governed by the app you share to.

Purchases

SnapBite Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; SnapBite does not see or store your card or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Network use

SnapBite itself uses the network only for Google Play Billing and to open web links (such as this policy) in your browser. Google Play services and the ML Kit libraries included in the app are part of Google's software; they may exchange technical information (for example diagnostics and model management) with Google under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). This information is not received by the developer.

Permissions

  • Camera (required): to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos.
  • Microphone (only when recording video with sound): to record audio.
  • Location (optional, off by default, requested only when you turn on location tagging): to write coordinates into your photos.
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to buy and check SnapBite Pro through Google Play.
  • Internet and network state: used by Google Play Billing and web links.

You can change or revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings > Apps > SnapBite > Permissions.

Wear OS companion app

The Wear OS remote talks to SnapBite on your phone only through the Wearable Data Layer (Bluetooth or Wi-Fi between your watch and your phone). Camera commands and the live viewfinder preview travel between your own devices. Nothing is stored on a server, and the watch app has no account or ads.

Retention and deletion

SnapBite keeps no data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete remotely. Data on your device can be removed at any time: reset the app's settings and learned suggestions from the in-app Settings, clear the app's data in Android Settings > Apps > SnapBite > Storage, or uninstall the app. Photos and videos you saved to your gallery are your files; delete them in your gallery app. For purchase and subscription records, contact Google Play.

Children

SnapBite is not directed to children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal data, no information is collected from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to process data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
