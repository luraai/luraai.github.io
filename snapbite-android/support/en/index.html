Frequently asked questions

Why does SnapBite ask for permissions?

  • Camera is required to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos.
  • Microphone is used only when you record a video with sound.
  • Location is optional and off by default. SnapBite asks for it only when you turn on location tagging, and writes the coordinates into the EXIF data of your own photos on the device.
  • SnapBite does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Photos are saved through Android MediaStore, and photos you edit are chosen with the system Photo Picker.

You can change permissions at any time in Android Settings > Apps > SnapBite > Permissions.

Filters do not show, or the preview is black

  • Filters are drawn live on your phone's GPU and need a real device with a working camera. Emulators and some virtual cameras cannot show them.
  • Close other apps that use the camera, then reopen SnapBite.
  • Check for app and system updates in Google Play and Android Settings, and restart the phone.
  • Only 3 looks plus Original are free. You can preview other looks in the viewfinder, but taking a photo with a Pro look needs SnapBite Pro.

Manual controls (ISO, shutter, white balance, focus) are missing

Manual controls depend on what your phone's camera exposes to apps. Some phones offer only some of them. Lens chips (ultra-wide, 1x, 2x) also appear only when the phone exposes those lenses. White balance in Kelvin and manual focus with focus peaking are Pro features.

How do purchases and restoring work?

SnapBite Pro is sold through Google Play as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. There is no free trial. SnapBite checks your Google Play purchases when it starts, so after reinstalling or switching phones, sign in with the same Google account, make sure you are online and open the app again.

  • Cancel a subscription: Google Play app > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > SnapBite.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

Video

The filter you choose is baked into the recorded video. Sound is recorded only if you allow the microphone.

Where are my photos and how do I share or export?

Photos are saved in Pictures/SnapBite and videos in Movies/SnapBite, and they appear in your gallery app. When you share a photo, location and other metadata are removed by default (you can change this in Settings). Editor export is up to 2048 px; full-size export needs SnapBite Pro.

Which languages are supported?

English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, German and French. SnapBite follows your phone's language settings. On Android 13 and later you can also choose a language for SnapBite in Android Settings > Apps > SnapBite > Language.

Wear OS remote does not connect

  • Pair the watch with your phone in the Wear OS app and keep Bluetooth on.
  • Install SnapBite on both the phone and the watch (Google Play).
  • Open SnapBite on the phone, then open the SnapBite app on the watch.
  • Shutter control is free. Live viewfinder, video, self-timer, camera flip, crown zoom and focus, and the filter picker need SnapBite Pro.
  • If it still fails, restart both devices. The watch and phone communicate only over the Wearable Data Layer.

How do I turn off photo tags?

Open Settings in SnapBite and switch off photo tags. Tags that were already saved remain in those photo files' description field; edit or remove them in your gallery app.

Privacy and data deletion requests

SnapBite has no accounts and no servers, so the developer holds no personal data about you. To remove data from your device, reset the app in Settings, clear its data in Android Settings > Apps > SnapBite > Storage, or uninstall the app. If you still want to make a deletion or privacy request, email contact@luraai.io and we will respond.
