Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026

Alibitale has no account and no server. What you ask and say is processed and stored only on your device, and AI lines are generated on your device. There are no ads or analytics SDKs.

1. Information we collect

None. LuraAI (“we”) does not collect personal information through Alibitale (“the app”).

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • Free-text questions, conversation logs and progress are stored only on your device and are never sent to us (we operate no servers).

2. AI lines

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, suspects' lines are generated on the device by Apple's on-device language model (Foundation Models). Your raw question text is not passed to the model (only a topic label interpreted by the app is used), and nothing leaves your device. The app, not the model, decides the answer, clues and truth; the model only supplies wording. AI-written lines carry an “AI-generated” label, and if you dislike one you can tap “Report” to replace it immediately. Reports are recorded only on your device; if you choose to, you can review and send an email draft yourself (nothing is sent automatically).

3. When the network is used

The app talks to Apple's App Store when you buy or restore the case pack. Apple handles payment; the app never sees payment details. If you are signed in to Game Center, achievements are handled by Apple.

4. iCloud sync

Only a progress summary (solved cases and star ratings) may sync through your own iCloud key-value store. Conversations and question text are not synced. Apple stores this data; we cannot access it.

5. Selling and sharing

Since we collect no personal information, there is nothing to share or sell. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or cross-app tracking.

6. Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app deals with crime-mystery themes, so please follow its age rating.

7. Deletion

In Settings › Data you can delete conversation logs or reset all progress. Deleting the app removes all data from the device. The iCloud summary is removed when you reset.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and significant changes are noted in the app's update notes.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@luraai.io
