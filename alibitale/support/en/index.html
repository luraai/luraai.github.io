How to play

How do I solve a case?

Question the three suspects with topic chips or your own words. Every statement becomes a thread in the Tale Loom. Drag a statement onto evidence (or another statement) to find pairs that clash; when one does, the thread snaps and the suspect wavers. Not every liar is the culprit, so name the culprit with evidence to back it up.

How do questions and stars work?

Only questions that get an answer use up your count. Repeating a question, being asked to clarify and correct contradictions are free; a wrong connection costs one. Fewer questions, accurate grounds and fewer hints earn more stars.

What is a “tell”?

Each suspect has a habit that shows when they lie (stammering, needless detail, changing the subject, suddenly stiff speech). But innocent people can lie too, to protect a secret.

AI

Which devices get AI lines?

Devices with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26 or later). On other devices you play every case to the end with choice-based questioning; difficulty and star rules are identical.

How do I turn AI off or report a line?

Turn AI lines off in Settings. Tap “Report” under an AI-generated line to replace it immediately. Reports stay on your device.

Purchases

What do I pay for?

The app (paid) includes two cases. Case Pack Vol. 1 (three more cases) is a one-time in-app purchase. There are no ads.

How do I restore purchases?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore purchases in Settings.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data

Where is my data?
