Socra is an offline coding practice app with an on-device AI tutor. Need help? Write to contact@luraai.io and include your device model, Android version and app version.

Getting started

  1. Open a problem on the Practice or Learn tab, write your code and tap Run.
  2. Stuck? Tap Hint. The tutor gives a question first, then the concept, the place in your code and a small fix. The solution opens after a few tries.
  3. Use Templates to watch algorithms run step by step, or run your own code in the visualizer. On any step tap "Why did it move this way?" and the tutor asks you guiding questions about the values at that moment.
  4. Settings > Study tools has your mistake notebook (failed tests grouped by kind, with review cards) and My problems, where you write or import problems of your own. In the editor you can also turn on "Predict the result before visualizing" to guess the output first.

Free and Pro

Free: the first two Python units (10 problems), basic hints, one AI hint per day, 6 algorithm visualizations, 3 visualizations of your own code per day, 3 problems of your own (Python), the mistake notebook and 2 AI Lab demos. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that opens everything. Restore it on the purchase screen if you reinstall or change device (same Google account).

Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet? No. Running code, the tutor and the visualizer work offline. Internet is only used for optional GitHub sync, optional model downloads and Google Play purchases.

Which AI does the tutor use? Gemini Nano (AICore) on supported devices. On other devices it uses an optional downloadable on-device model (Settings > AI tutor) or the prepared step-by-step hints.

Which languages run? Python, JavaScript, SQL, Lua, Ruby (mruby) and C (a C11 subset interpreter). Some advanced language features are not available.

Where is my data? Only on your device, including your mistake notebook and your own problems (they are not part of Android backup; export a problem as JSON from My problems if you want a copy). See the privacy policy.

The tutor only shows a caption and a question on a step. That is what you get on a device without an on-device AI model. On supported devices (Gemini Nano) or with a downloaded model, the tutor answers with guiding questions about the step's values.

How do I send feedback? Settings > Send feedback opens your email app addressed to contact@luraai.io.

Nearby pair coding does not find my friend. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network without client isolation, with Socra open on the Pair screen.

Wear OS app. Install it from Google Play on your watch. It works on its own; connect it to the phone app to sync progress and use the remote.

Refunds. Handled by Google Play according to its refund policy.
