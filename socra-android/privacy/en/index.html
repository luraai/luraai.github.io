Socra Privacy Policy (Android)

Last updated: 2026-10-17

English 한국어

Socra is an offline coding practice app by Luraai. This policy explains how the Android version (phone and Wear OS) handles data. In short: we do not collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Socra has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your code, questions or progress.

What stays on your device

  • Your code, drafts, solved problems, attempts, hints used and practice days
  • Tutor conversations and settings, including questions about a single step of your own run (the step's variable values are only read by the on-device tutor)
  • Your mistake notebook (failed tests grouped by kind, missed predictions) and the problems you write or import in "My problems"
  • Projects and files you create
  • Optional downloaded on-device model files
  • A GitHub token, if you choose to connect GitHub. It is encrypted with the Android Keystore, stored only on your device and excluded from backups.

All code execution, grading, the AI tutor and the visualizer run on your device. Your code and conversations never leave it.

Optional features that use the network

Only when you use them, and always over HTTPS:

  • GitHub (Pro): when you connect, push or pull a project, Socra talks directly to GitHub with your own account. We never see it.
  • Model download (Pro): when you tap download, an open-weight model file is fetched from huggingface.co. Nothing about you is sent except what any web request contains (such as your IP address).
  • Purchases: Google Play handles payment for the one-time "Pro Lifetime" purchase. We do not receive your payment details.

Backup and export

If you enable Google backup on your device, Android may back up Socra's progress, projects and settings to your Google account, under Google's terms. We cannot access it. You can also export and import your progress with Android's file picker. The mistake notebook and your own problems are not included in Android's backup or in the progress export; they stay on the device where you made them, and you can export a single problem as a JSON file yourself.

Feedback

Settings > Send feedback only opens your email app with our address (contact@luraai.io) and the app version in the subject. Socra sends nothing by itself; what you send is up to you.

Nearby pair coding

Pair coding with a friend works over the same Wi-Fi network directly between the two devices. Shared code and chat go to the other device you approved, not to us. The AI pair runs on your device.

Wear OS

The watch app stores progress and quiz state on the watch and syncs with your phone through Google's Wearable Data Layer. No sensors or location are used.

Children

Socra is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any personal information.

Deleting your data

Use Settings in the app (reset progress, delete projects, delete models, clear the mistake notebook, delete your own problems) or uninstall the app. Because we hold no data on servers, there is nothing else to delete. To remove a Google backup, use your Google account backup settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
