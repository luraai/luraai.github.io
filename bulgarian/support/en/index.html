Frequently asked questions

How do I start the course?

On first launch the app asks for a start date, your health conditions and, optionally, your bag weights, and the 12-week starter course runs from that day. Those 12 weeks are one season; the course continues through 13 seasons (156 weeks, three years). You can change the start date in Settings, or postpone the course and repeat a season after a break. A Bulgarian bag is required separately.

Are the 3-year course and season 2 onward free?

Season 1 (the 12-week starter course), repeat routines, bag weight ranges, the timer and the log are free. Seasons 2–13 need Plus. From season 2 the plan is not a study-backed course but a programming example built from the same moves and safety rules, and the app labels it that way.

What are repeat routines?

In the Course tab, open Repeat routines for 15-, 25- and 40-minute day routines and a 2–3×/week maintenance routine. They repeat the same moves and do not affect course progress or attendance.

What are my bag weights used for?

Enter your light and heavy bag weights in Settings › My bag weights and exercises, reps and rest adapt to your range (~7 kg, 8–12 kg, 13–19 kg, 20 kg+). The ranges are for reference, based on the maker's recommended weights; leave them empty to see the course as written. Do not start with too heavy a bag.

How do I watch the motion guides?

Tap a move in a session or the timer tab to open its vector motion. You can switch the view (front, angle, side), play at 0.5× or 1×, and turn on the bag path, correct vs. common mistakes, working muscles, center of mass and joint angles. The “Move library” below the Course tab lists every move.

Where do I change the male or female figure?

Settings › Motion guide model. It does not change the course; entering your light and heavy bag weights adjusts the bag size and the center-of-mass calculation.

What do the evidence grades R, A, C and E mean?

R: measured for that move in a study. A: borrowed from a similar move such as a kettlebell swing. C: manufacturer or coaching sources. E: an estimate. No study measures bag-specific moves, so these are diagrams to help you learn; adjust your real position to your body and bag weight.

I want less movement.

Turn on Settings › Accessibility › Motion › Reduce Motion on your iPhone and the motions stop playing; use the step panels and slider instead.

How does the AI coach work?

It uses Apple Intelligence if available, otherwise the model you downloaded in Settings › Open-source AI model, otherwise rule-based text. Everything runs on your device, and suggestions stay inside the safety rules (RPE 9+ at most twice a week, 48 hours between hard sessions, no load increases after pain).

How do I delete the open-source model?

Open Settings › Open-source AI model and tap Delete. The storage is freed immediately.

I used up my free AI.

Free AI is 5 uses a month and resets on the 1st. Plus (monthly or yearly subscription, or lifetime) has no limit. The course, timer, log and rule-based coach notes stay free.

Restoring purchases and cancelling

Tap “Restore purchases” on the Plus screen. Cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

My Watch log isn't on my iPhone.

Logs transfer automatically when the iPhone and Watch are near each other and the app has been opened once. If they aren't connected, the log is delivered the next time they connect.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes all records on this device. Nothing is stored on a server.

Is this medical advice?

No. It is an exercise guide, not medical advice. Stop immediately if you feel pain or lose balance, and talk to a doctor before starting if you have a medical condition.
