Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 October 2026

Summary

Bulgarian Bag Training does not collect personal data. Your workout logs, re-test numbers and health-condition choices stay on this device only. There is no developer server, and no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

What is stored on your device

  • Course start date, session logs (RPE, time, pain), re-test numbers (grip, push-ups, standing long jump, resting heart rate, spin count)
  • Health-condition choices (shoulder, back, blood pressure and more) and settings (timer sound, haptics, AI choice, motion-guide figure (male/female), light and heavy bag weights)
  • This information is removed when you delete the app. The developer cannot access it.

Apple Watch and Health

The Apple Watch app reads your heart rate during a workout and saves the circuit as a workout in the Health app, only if you allow it. This data is used only on your device and is not sent to or shared with the developer or any third party. The app works without heart rate if you decline. You can change access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices. Session logs move between iPhone and Watch only through Apple's device-to-device connection (WatchConnectivity).

The iPhone app reads resting heart rate, HRV and sleep from the Health app read-only (it writes nothing), and only if you turn it on in Settings. The comparison of recent days with your usual values is used only to word a note in the weekly review; it never leaves the device and is not used to judge your health.

Camera, microphone and motion sensors

  • Camera (form check): on only while you run a check. Only joint-angle numbers from your side view are computed; video and photos are not saved or sent.
  • Microphone (spoken notes): on only when you dictate a body-state note. Speech is turned into text on this device with Apple's on-device recognition and the audio is not stored. The resulting note stays on this device.
  • Watch motion sensors (spin test): wrist motion is recorded only during the 3-minute spin test. Raw recordings (CSV) stay on the two devices, and the iPhone keeps only the latest 5. Nothing is sent to a server.

AI features

  • Apple Intelligence: runs on Apple's on-device model on supported devices. What you enter is not sent to the developer.
  • Open-source model (Qwen3, Apache-2.0): runs on your device after you consent and download it in Settings. Only the model file is downloaded from Hugging Face; no workout or health information is uploaded. During the download, Hugging Face can see ordinary connection details such as your IP address. You can delete the model at any time in Settings.
  • AI gives exercise-guide suggestions only; it is not medical advice.

Purchases

Plus subscriptions and the lifetime option are handled by Apple in-app purchase. Apple manages payment details; the developer never sees your card information. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Sharing and tracking

We do not share or sell user information, and we do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

Children

This app is not directed to children and does not collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will say so on this page and in the update notes. Contact: contact@luraai.io
