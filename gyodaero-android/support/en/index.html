FAQ

How do I change my shift pattern? Calendar › the repeat button at the top right › tap the chips in Pattern settings, then tap "Fill a year of shifts". Days you changed by hand (★) are kept.

How do I enter a cover shift or overtime? Long-press a date and choose the shift. The free version allows 2 patterns; quick edits are PRO.

My alarm didn't ring. In Settings › Apps › Gyodaero check that notifications are on, the "Alarms & reminders" permission is allowed and the alarm volume is not zero. Also make sure battery saver isn't stopping the app. Shift-linked alarms are a PRO feature.

I bought PRO but don't see it on my new phone. Open the app's Settings › Restore purchase. With the same Google account it is restored at no extra cost.

I switched phones. What about my schedule? If Android backup is on, it is restored on the new device. Or save a backup file (calendar_backup.json) in Settings › Backup & restore and tap "Restore from backup" on the new device.

The pay estimate differs from my payslip. Check hourly wage, night multiplier and holiday premium in Pay › Settings against your contract. The result is an estimate for reference.

How do I add a widget? Long-press an empty spot on the Home screen › Widgets › Gyodaero. Today and Text widgets are free; Countdown, Week and Month widgets are PRO.

What about the Wear OS watch app? Open Gyodaero on your phone once and your schedule syncs to the watch. Install the app from the Play Store on the watch and add the Tile or a complication.

I want a refund. Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Google Play: Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history.
