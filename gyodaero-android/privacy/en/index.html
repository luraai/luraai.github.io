Gyodaero Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026 · Applies to: Gyodaero (Android, Wear OS) · Contact: contact@luraai.io

English 한국어

Gyodaero doesn't collect personal information, and it doesn't send any to a server.

1. Information we collect

The developer collects no information. There is no sign-up or login, and the app uses no advertising or analytics SDKs. The information in section 2 is stored only on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Shift patterns, work records, and changes such as cover shifts and overtime
  • Events you add yourself and calendar settings (name, color, visibility)
  • Alarm time settings, pay calculation settings (hourly wage, premium multipliers, etc.) and results
  • Theme, widget and sticker settings
  • If you use Care, your condition log: mood and energy, sleep time, caffeine and activity, status tags and notes you write yourself. This may include sensitive information related to mental health.
  • Whether you agreed to the Care notice and the date (kept per device)

3. Android backup

If Android backup (your Google account) is turned on, the operating system may back up your schedule and settings data (the app's state.json) to your Google account. Google manages that backup and the Gyodaero developer cannot access it. You can turn it off any time in your device settings. Care consent and PRO purchase status are not carried over by backups.

4. Care · Condition log (for reference)

The Care guide is general wellness information calculated on your device from your schedule and the entries you have logged. It is not a substitute for professional medical or psychological counseling, diagnosis or treatment. Noticing signs of distress in a note also happens only on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. You can withdraw your consent and delete saved condition entries at any time in Settings › Care notice & consent. Health data sources (Health Connect, sensors) are not used.

5. Device calendar (optional)

Only if you allow it, the app uses the Calendar permissions (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR) to read events on your device and, if you choose, to write your shifts to a calendar named "Gyodaero Shifts". Events from Google accounts added to your device appear through the device calendar. Gyodaero does not receive or store your Google sign-in information, and events never leave your device.

6. Backup file · CSV export

The backup (calendar_backup.json) and pay report (salary_report.csv) are created only when you request them, and you choose where they are saved and who to share them with. What happens after you share them is governed by that service's policy.

7. Notifications · alarms · widgets

Notification permission is used only to deliver shift alarms, day-before reminders and event reminders, all scheduled on your device. Widgets and the Wear OS watch app exchange the same schedule data directly between your phone and your connected watch using Google's Wearable Data Layer, without any developer server.

8. In-app purchases

Gyodaero PRO (lifetime) purchases are processed by Google Play. Gyodaero does not receive your payment information. The Google Play Billing library is used to verify the purchase.

9. Children's privacy

Gyodaero is intended for working adults. Because we don't collect personal information, we don't collect children's personal information either.

10. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Data backed up to your Google account can be deleted in your device's Google backup settings. Inside the app you can delete condition entries in Settings › Care notice & consent.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will let you know in the app's update notes and on this page.
